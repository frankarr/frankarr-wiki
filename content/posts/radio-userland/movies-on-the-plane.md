Saturday, 25 February 2006 Movies on the plane I'm over in Seattle at the moment, attending a bunch of meetings. I'm based at The Willows Lodge The trip over was long and tiring. On the Sydney to Los Angeles leg (14.5 hours), I got to watch 3 movies: 40 Year Old Virgin I really enjoyed this movie. Sure, the language was crude but it felt authentic. Steve Carell was great, and I find Catherine Keener really hot. I've had a thing for her since Being John Malkovich . Don't know why. Capote All I can say is " WOW ". This is a pretty intense movie. Philip Seymour Hoffman *WAS* Truman Capote . By co-incidence, this also had Catherine Keener in the movie playing Nelle Harper Lee , the author of " To Kill A Mockingbird ", one of my all time favourite book & movie. Walk The Line Another BioPic, and another " WOW ". Joaquin Phoenix *WAS* Johnny Cash. The music was wonderful. I really got into that Johnny Cash vibe. It's inspired me to go and find the soundtrack Time to rest.... 7:45:17 PM
