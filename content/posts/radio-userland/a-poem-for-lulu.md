Friday, 15 July 2005 A poem for LuLu A poem from my sister Rita, the Digital Diva Lulu Lulu Lulu You are such a cute shihpoo We loved you because u were so lovable We hope you find a lovely soft spot In the special place in dog heaven For cute lulu shihpoo You are sadly missed by Antony , Billy, Emma and Brice Our mother Moira, who loved lulu the cute shihpoo Will always have a place in her heart For the lulu who stole our hearts Our father Frank, who blogged about his new baby Will keep her memory alive on his blog Lulu we never lost you because u will live on forever in the Blog and in our Hearts [listening to: Gimme the Car - Violent Femmes ] 8:22:58 PM
