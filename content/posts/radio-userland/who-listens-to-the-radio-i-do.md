Tuesday, 16 August 2005
Who Listens to the Radio? I Do
A new radio station started in Sydney in the past few weeks. It's called
Vega 95.3
from
DMG Radio Australia
(they guys behind
Nova 96.9
) and
it is aimed at the 'Over 40' market
with presenters such as
Angela Catterns
,
Wendy Harmer
,
Francis Leach
,
Tony Squires & Rebecca Wilson
. The
new station has brought some controversy with it
.
So, out of curiosity, I tuned in this afternoon and listened the the drive show with
Tony & Rebecca
. It was like an old episode of
The Fat when it was still on the ABC
. I liked it. The music was more oldies than I would have liked, but the banter was entertaining.
So, I have a new routine. In my morning commute I will continue to listen to
Merrick and Rosso on Nova969
and for my evening commute I'm going to switch from
Richard Glover
on
ABC 702
and start listening to
Tony & Rebecca
.
I bet you are wondering why I'm not listening to
podcasts
or
cds
in my car - well my commute is pretty short and it's the only time I get to hear any news during the day. I save all my
podcasts
and
cds
for longer trips, or for listening in the office when I'm at work.
[listening to: Run -
Ben Lee
