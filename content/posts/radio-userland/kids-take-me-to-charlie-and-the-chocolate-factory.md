Sunday, 4 September 2005
Kids take me to Charlie and the Chocolate Factory
Today was Father's Day.
My kids let me sleep in until 10.30am, and then they brought me breakfast in bed, and some lovely gifts.
Following our
regular lunch at Leichardt
, the boys took me to see the recently opened
Charlie and the Chocolate Factory
. Emma didn't come with us, she sees it next week with her friends at Amy's birthday party.
The movie was excellent.
Johnny Depp
was terrific as the disturbed Willy Wonka, abd Bricey loved the Oompa Loompas (who were all played by the same actor). I was surprised to see aussie actor
Noah Taylor
playing Charlie's dad.
Tim Burton
has another quirky hit. But don't take my word for it -
even The SMH liked it
