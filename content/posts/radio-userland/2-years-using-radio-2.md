Bloody Volvo Driver
This week we became members of the
Bloody Volvo Drivers
club. We traded in Moira's Honda Odyssey for a new Volvo XC90 2.5T.
We picked the car up last night from
Trivetts Classic Cars
in Surry Hills. We had been looking for a car for a while and had been to a
Volvo dealer closer to us
, but they didnt do anything to win our business, so we treked to the other side of town.
Surprisingly the Trivetts salesperson said this wasn't an isolated incident and he had seen a number of customers who had come from that
particular dealer
in the past few weeks. This is really surprising, since it's not a cheap car and I am sure the commission would be pretty healthly. They just expected the car to sell itself. Bloody Volvo Sales People from Alto Volvo, thanks for nothing!
After picking up the car, we took the kids to Fox Studios for dinner and I bumped into
Bruce McLeod
and his lovely wife. Hi Bruce - good to see you again!
