Wednesday, 25 February 2004 Adventures with Orkut So, its been 2 weeks since I started with Orkut , and a few things have changed since my last update. These are the stats: I am connected to 39457 people through 34 friends, and I have another 20 people who I have invited, but who haven't responded. I've noticed that a bunch of invites I sent last week have just gotten through, so I expect there is lots of email backed up. Some other stats: profile views: 126 , average path: 3.7 , number of friends: 55 . These are interesting, but there should be some other pieces of data, like number of degress to orkut himself. I can see the communities and bulletin boards, but i would like to see more - like suggestion people who are similar, suggestion communities to join, even recommending music,movies or books based on interests, etc. It will be interesting to see how it pans out. 8:30:53 PM
