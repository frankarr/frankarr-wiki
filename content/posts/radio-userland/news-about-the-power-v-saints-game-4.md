Impressions about the Gold Coast
I've just come back from a week on the
Gold Coast
with the family - we spent 3 days at the
Hyatt Sanctuary Cove
and then 3 days at
Palazzo Versace
.
While at
Sanctuary Cove
, we went to 2 theme parks -
Movie World
and
Wet N' Wild
, as well as had a lounge around at the resort. It was nice to sit by the pool, watching the kids play.
We saw most of
the attractions at Movie World
-
Shrek 4D AdventureTM
,
Scooby-Doo Spooky Coaster
,
Road Runner Rollercoaster
,
Wild West Falls Adventure Ride
,
Looney Tunes Village
,
Looney Tunes River Ride
,
Lethal Weapon - The Ride
,
Batman Adventure - The Ride 2
. I chickened out and didn't go on the
Superman
Escape
ride.
Wet N' Wild
wasn't as much fun, mainly due to Emma's tantrum and she wouldn't go on any of the
rides
. While she sulked, Brice and I tackled Terror Canyon, Mammoth Falls, Calypso Beach and hung out at Buccaneer Bay. He loved it and would have stayed there all day.
Following
the theme park adventures
, we moved to our other hotel, the
Palazzo
. That afternoon, we ventured into
Surfer's Paradise
for lunch at the
Hard Rock Cafe
and a visit
the Ripley museum
. We even spotted a
meter maid
or two!
The following day, we tackled another theme park -
Sea World
. I reckon this is the best of the bunch. A mix of
rides
,
attractions
,
animal adventures
, not to mention
Sharks
and
Polar Bears
. We spent a whole day there, and we only got to see half of the park.
After all the excitement of
the theme parks
, I took the kids back to
Surfers
to ride the
Aqua Duck
, an
amphibious vehicle
that trundels around the streets of Surfers and then glides along the broadwater
. The kids even got to drive the Duck when it was on the water! We then did our own trundeling on the streets and went to the
Gold Coast Wax Museum
. There is only one word to describe this place - LAME! It's a bit of an
anachronism
amongst the other attractions on the Gold Coast. I should have gone to play
mini golf
at
King Tutts Putt Putt
We wrapped up the trip with some rest time at
Palazzo Versace
. It was nice, but the overall place had a weird vibe - lots of "beautiful people" strutting in their expensive clothes and
refurbished bodies
. The word of the day for me was "
Faux
". Definitely not my scene. As nice as the place looked, I was glad to be leaving it and heading back home to my own little resort,
So, there you have it - a week on the
Gold Coast
. Photos are coming soon. I need to pull them off the camera.
It was nice to go away, but it's much nicer to be home. I have another week left of my holidays, no email, no meetings, but I've got a bunch of things I need to get sorted.
