Wednesday, 25 January 2006 elvis costello rocks the sydney opera house We went to see Elvis Costello play with the Sydney Symphony at the Sydney Opera House. Again, he rocked. I am always amazed by the Sydney Opera House whenever I go there. it is a truly amazing piece of architecture. We are so lucky to have this beautiful building in our own back yard. Anyways, the concert was great. the first part was a performance from Il Sogno, which was was written for the Italian Dance Company, Aterballetto, for their adaptation of A Midsummer Nights Dream .  The second part Elvis performs compositions that he has arranged for orchestra, together with songs arranged by Burt Bacharach, Bill Frisell, Steve Nieve and Vince Mendoza. It was amazing. [listening to: Alibi - Elvis Costello ] 10:36:05 AM
