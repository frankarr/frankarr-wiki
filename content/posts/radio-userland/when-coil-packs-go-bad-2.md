When Coil Packs Go Bad
I was driving to work this morning in
my Audi
and it felt really really rough.
The car made weird noises, it vibrated as I was driving, it had a horrible idle, it laboured as I accelerated. It just didn't feel right.
I called the good people at
Scotts Audi
and described the symptoms. The dude seemed like he had seen this problem before and told me to take the car into the
Service Center
asap.
I dropped the car off. The guy said that it was "misfiring". Hmmm, something to do with the
spark plugs
maybe?
The dude had also mentioned Coil Packs. Hmmm - what's that? I did a search and
found the following
What are coil packs?
A coil pack sits on top of each cylinder of the engine and serves as the ignition coil to fire the cylinder. Coil packs have been used for a number of years and by a number of different manufacturers.
Makes sense. Definitly related to the spark plugs.
Reading
the info on that site
was a bit of a worry. I spent the morning fretting about my little 4 wheeled friend..
The guys called me back a bit after midday with good news. It indeed was a failed Coil Pack and now it's fixed.
Cost to Frank = $0
w00t! Gotta love warranty work!
