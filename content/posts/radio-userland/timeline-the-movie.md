Timeline - the movie
After reading
Timeline
, I was really looking forward to the
movie
.
I took
Antony
to see it on Friday evening at
Macquarie
It is tough to summarise a book with such rich narrative into less than 2 hours and this movie is living proof of that. It had the elements of the story, but none of the interesting bits. And the casting was pretty poor. The character of Chris Johnston was played by
Paul Walker
- this dude can't act! And the character of André Marek was played by Mick Molloy look-alike
Gerard Butler
.
All in all, a pretty B-Grade movie and
Kerry agrees
.
