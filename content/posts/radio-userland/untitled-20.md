Sunday, 23 November 2003 Mobile Web Services Enter the Mobile Web Services Developer Contest Microsoft and Vodafone are seeking innovative developer applications that showcase the use of mobile Web services and span across PCs and mobile devices. Top prizes include a $5,000 award and round trip travel to the Microsoft Mobile Developer Conference in Spring 2004. The finalists will also have their applications featured in several Vodafone and Microsoft industry events, and as an added incentive Vodafone may deploy the winning applications into their markets. Registration is limited to the first 250 eligible entries received. If you've got what it takes, register for the Mobile Web Services Developer Contest now!
