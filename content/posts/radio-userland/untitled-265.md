A House fit for a King?
We did some "open house" touring this weekemd. Moira has got the moving bug again.
One of the places we went to see was a riot! It had a name -
Camelot
and a tag line - "A house fit for a King".
What was truly bizarre was the interior, It reminded me of one of
Saddam Hussein's Palaces
.
As we looked around the house we saw photos of the "Man Of The House" with
John Howard
(yes, the Prime Minister), and as we wandered around, the photos were more bizarre including one of the "Man Of The House" in full milirary uniform and a Gun. I wanted to take a photo, but the estate agent was nearby.
We reckon this house belonged to an Iraqi official,
maybe an envoy that assisted the AWB who had recently left the country
. It definitly had that feel to it.
If you are in Sydney, go check it out. The address is
96 Livingstone Avenue, Pymble
