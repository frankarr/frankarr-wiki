Nerds FC : Great TV
We are really enjoying a program on
SBS
called
Nerds FC
.
The premise -
take 14 nerds, teach them to play soccer and have them play a professional team
. Yes, it's a reality tv show, but it is really really good.
We're up to
episode 3 this week where they record a team song and film a video clip
- very entertaining seeing them dancing with the brazillian samba dancers.
The
show's website
is also good - it has
an rss feed
and offers
podcasts
, as well as
clips from the show
.
Very progressive SBS! Where's the lyrics to the team song???
BTW - SBS is going to be repeating the program later in the year, if you have missed the first 3 episodes.
