Monday, 29 November 2004 Arrested Developed *finally* on TV in Australia I read today that Arrested Development will *finally* make it to television here in Australia. I got series 1 of Arrested Development on DVD while I was off work , and have seen all the episodes . Do yourself a favour and watch this program. It is the best program you will have seen all year!! Channel 7 will be showing it on Tuesdays at 10.35pm, which is an insult to this program. This is just typical of this network - they did the same thing to [scrubs] and Will & Grace . And let's not mention how they killed Tony Squires' career . I hate this network as much as I hate Channel 9 , who destroyed Six Feet Under & The West Wing just to name a few. Thank goodness for DVD!
