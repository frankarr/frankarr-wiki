Friday, 1 October 2004
Holiday Day 4 :More of Melbourne
The kids decided to lie in bed for 30 minutes more than the previous morning. Ah bliss!
We headed of the Brunetti again - everyone likes that place. After our late breakfast, we dropped Moira back at the hotel to have a facial and Antony to play Star Wars Battlefield on his XBOX. I took all the other kids to Elwood beach for a scooter along the bike track and a run at the local playground. We had lunch at the Elwood Beach House, another favourite eating place
The boys and girls split up and I took my boys back to the hotel for a splash in the pool The girls went shopping down Acland St.
We ended the night back at my dad's house for a family dinner before we head off on the road to Narooma the next day
