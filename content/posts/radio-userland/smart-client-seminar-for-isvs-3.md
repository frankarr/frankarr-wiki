Thursday, 13 November 2003 Smart Client Seminar for ISVs Microsoft Smart Client Seminar for ISVs - Sydney (3-5 Dec 03) and Melbourne (9-11 Dec 03) Seize the growing opportunities for your smart client software applications now by registering to attend the Microsoft Smart Client Seminar for ISVs near you. This unique developer event will provide you with the in-depth knowledge and training you need to start building smart client applications on Microsoft technologies. Smart client applications present unprecedented opportunities for ISVs. By 2005 as much as 60% of all new .NET applications are expected to be designed with smart client deployment in mind. (Source: "The Return of Client/Server - or, at least, Rich Clients," Gartner) The Microsoft Smart Client Seminar for ISVs , designed by Microsoft technologists and delivered by industry-leading trainers, will help you to seize these opportunities by providing you with technical sessions, demos, and hands-on labs. This unique 3 day training event is being held in Melbourne on 9-11 December 03 and in Sydney on 3-5th December 03 . For other states, additional sessions will be held in early 2004. No existing knowledge of Microsoft smart client technologies is required to participate, but familiarity with the Microsoft .NET platform and XML Web services will be beneficial.
