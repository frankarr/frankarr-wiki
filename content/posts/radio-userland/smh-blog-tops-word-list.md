Thursday, 2 December 2004 SMH: Blog tops word list Saw an interesting aticle in the SMH today - it carries a story that the word Blog is the most looked up word on the internet A four-letter term that came to symbolise the difference between old and new media during this year's presidential campaign tops US dictionary publisher Merriam-Webster's list of the 10 words of the year. Merriam-Webster said on Tuesday that blog, defined as "a Web site that contains an online personal journal with reflections, comments and often hyperlinks", was one of the most looked-up words on its Internet sites this year.
