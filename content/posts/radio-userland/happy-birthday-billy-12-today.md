Thursday, 11 August 2005
Happy Birthday Billy - 12 Today
Today is my
little Bill's birthday
. He turns 12.
Actually he's not that little. He is taller than his mother and has feet like a hobbit.
Bill has a blog on MSN Spaces
but he hasn't updated it for a while - maybe this will inspire him.
He scored well on his birthday - we got him a
Zen Touch
with a 20gb harddisk. It won't take too long to fill that up. I also got him all the INXS back catalog - he has become a big fan since watching
Rockstar
on Fox8. His brothers and sisters all got him some cool stuff too, but I hope he gets a chance to blog about it himself.
My big gift is coming soon - when he was younger, I promised him a ride on the
JetBoat at Sydney Harbour
. The youngest you can be is 12 so I told him when he turned 12, I would take him out on the boat. I know what I'll be doing this weekend!
Happy Birthday Billy!!!
