2 Weeks Is A Long Time Not To Blog Yup, I haven't done a post in 2 weeks. You know, a lot has happened in that time. My wife got our house and kids all packed We sold our house in Sydney We sold our cars We spent out last few days in downtown Sydney We got onto UNITED 870 (COACH CLASS), sat in uncomfortable seats (58F 58H 58J 61F 62E 62F) and traveled 7412 miles across the world We landed in Seattle with 9 suitcases between us We arrived at our temporary location and discovered it a was small 3 bedroom apartment We waited for our broadband to be activated We started house hunting We opened a US bank account We went shopping Moira got a kettle We got new mobile phones I got a local SIM Card We went looking at cars Antony turned 16 We got Antony an Xbox 360 Elite for his Birthday We saw The Bourne Ultimatum I started work at my new job A welcome email went out welcoming me to the team We moved to a house that can fit my family We waited for our broadband to be activated We went shopping I bought a new car , thanks to the Amazing Auto Woman We signed up at Costco We continued house hunting I twittered a lot Phew - and that's just the tip of the iceberg. Lots of stories to follow But rest assured - we are returning to our regular posts!
