Tech.Ed Early Bird offer extended to 20 June The title says it all. The cutoff date for Tech.Ed 2006 Early Bird Group Discount tickets is now 20 June. Until then, there are substantial savings on the cost of providing your team with three days of exceptional training. Tech.Ed is an excellent investment in training and development in other ways. You can expect your staff to pick up information and knowledge which are immediately applicable in the workplace, and to upgrade their skills through highly technical hands-on training. There will be no be further extensions to the Early Bird rates after 20 June so register now to take advantage of the discount - and also to ensure team members have a place at popular workshops.
