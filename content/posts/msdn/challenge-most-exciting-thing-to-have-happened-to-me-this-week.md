Challenge : Most Exciting Thing to Have Happened to Me this Week I've been issued a challenge by Darren Neimke , who is feeling pretty cocky at the moment. The challenge, which I have to accept, is to write about the most exciting thing to have happened to me this week. Too easy. As I have mentioned earlier, I have been recouperating at home for the past few weeks , and have only just returned to work. The most exciting thing for the week was driving into the office on Thursday, and seeing the Ascend training underway - we had folks from all over attending the training, which was delivered by a mixture of my team ( Andew Coates, Jason McConnell , Charles Sterling, & Dave Glover) and local MVPs, such as Nick Weinholt, Greg Low, Peter Stanksi, Adam Cogan & Nick Randolph. I hope I haven't forgotten anyone. There were 3 rooms full of enthusiastic students. It was fantastic to see! Definitly exciting. Oh, the other exciting thing was getting my parcel from Amazon which contained the following DVDs: Arrested Development - Season 1 Fahrenheit 9/11 Greg the Bunny - The Complete Series Muffin Man Outfoxed - Rupert Murdoch's War on Journalism Super Size Me All good viewing - especially Arrested Development . This program hasn't been shown in Australia yet. It is the funniest program I have seen in years! I found a good review too. So, all in all, an exciting week!
