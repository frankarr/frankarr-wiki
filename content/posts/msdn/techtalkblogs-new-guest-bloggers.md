TechTalkBlogs : New Guest Bloggers I alluded to this last week , we will be introducing a new guest blogger on TechTalkBlogs. Well, actually it's more than one guest editor, we've got 3 ! A trio of TechTalkBloggers: Geoff Appleby, MVP, Canberra Resident, Family man , and an attendee for this year's TechEd Paul Glavich, MVP, Podcaster, ASP.NET dude , and a speaker at this year's TechEd Damian Edwards , aka Tech.ED How's that for a lineup! A Triumphant Trio!! The guys will be blogging their experiences in the leadup to TechEd . I am sure we'll see some innovations such as those brought in by Darren Neimke, our departing guest blogger.
