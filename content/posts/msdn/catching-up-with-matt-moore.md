Catching up with Matt Moore Today I finally caught up with fellow blogger, and Harold Lloyd look alike, Matt Moore. Matt had arranged a bloggers lunch a few months ago , but I missed it due to a family emergency. Today we met for lunch. at a local eatery and wifi hotspot near our respective offices. Matt has recently changed jobs, going from one mulitnational to another .  (We met for lunch, but I did all the eating). We talked blogging, podcasts, wikis, web 2.0 in australia, and the joys of working for multinationals. At the end of it all, we agreed to do it again, but this time with more people, and in more delightful surroundings. Possibly with lamingtons. Cheers Matt. Good to finally meet.
