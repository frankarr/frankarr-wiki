Silverlight Games
Spotted over on
Chris Bowen's Blog
, a
handy list of Silverlight Games
"Dr. Popper"
- Burst the matching bubbles, the more, the better.
"Digger"
- Sort of Dig-Dug meets Montezuma's Revenge.
"Zero Gravity"
- An addictive and well-made game! A nice example of the possibilities of the platform.
"Chess"
- Your basic chess, but shows how Silverlight 1.1 with .NET fares against a JavaScript-based AI.
"Destroy All Invaders"
- A cool mashup with Virtual Earth where you fly over the oblique angle view of a location of your choice while shooting UFOs.
"Silverlight Rocks!"
- A shoot the asteroids game.
"Sprawl"
- A good demonstration of potential with Silverlight 1.0.
