Wonderful WPF Apps : TrialBalance TrialBalance is a project being developed by Paul Stovell TrialBalance is a free, shared-source accounting application that is designed to teach basic double-entry bookkeeping to High School and University students. TrialBalance is not designed to run your business or to do your tax, although, if you knew enough about accounting, it could. TrialBalance simply provides a chart of accounts and allows you to record transactions against the five major account types - Assets, Liabilities, Revenue, Expenses and Equity It is a work in progress at the moment, but it's a slick looking WPF Application Nice one Paul Technorati tags: WPF, Trial Balance, Paul Stovell [ Current Listening to: Forgotten Years by Midnight Oil from 20,000 Watt R.S.L.: Greatest Hits ] Comments Anonymous November 29, 2006 PingBack from http://double-entry-bookkeeping.pierresccservice.com/wonderful-wpf-apps-trialbalance/ Anonymous December 08, 2006 I finally was able to logon to my BlogBeat account this weekend, following a PEBKAC sitution.
