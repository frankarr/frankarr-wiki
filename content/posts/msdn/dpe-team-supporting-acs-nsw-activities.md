DPE Team supporting ACS NSW activities
I got the latest
ACS NSW
e-News
newsletter (Issue 70) this evening, and was delighted to see two members of my
team (
Anna Liu
and
Jeff Alexander
) as guest speakers in
some upcoming sessions in June.
Our Enterprise Solutions Development SIG next Thursday the 15th of June
with Dr Anna Liu is an event not to be missed! Dr Liu will discuss how an
organization can improve its development approach inline with expectations
using "Software Factories". The next
generation approach to Enterprise Software Solution Development is based on
Domain Specific Languages, patterns frameworks and guidance to build
applications for specific industries or markets. The presentation will explain
how Visual Studio Team System 2005 can assist advanced software engineering
and research projects. It presents the Microsoft's vision of the future of
software development. Log on to our website for further details and
on-line
registration.
Don't forget to register for the Special Microsoft event on the 20th of
June in the Auditorium at the Sydney Mechanics School of Arts building. Our
special guest speakerJeff Alexander (IT Pro Evangelist) with Microsoft will
present and demonstrate the new
Microsoft VISTA operating System and
Office 12
. Jeff will give the same presentation on the 28th June in
the Conference room at the South Western Sydney Institute of Tafe,
Campbelltown Campus. Visit the
ACS
website for further details & on-line registration for the Sydney event or
visit the
SWSI
website to
register on-line for the Campbelltown event.
