Security Seminar/Interchange/Eforums I've been asked by different folks what exactly are we doing for the upcoming Security event in February/March. This year, we're approaching the format a little differently from previously and we are doing a number of things: One Day Paid-For Seminar Evening-based communirty focused Interchange Webcasts, Chats, Newsgroups, Blogs and other online eForum goodness We see this as an evolution of how we do events, so you go away with even more knowledge and insights, and continue to exchange ideas beyond a single day event. It's a way of continuing the conversation with Microsoft and amongst other members of the community. Also, we want this to be the way moving forward for future activities, a consistent way to get information from Microsoft. Yes, it's risky to try new things, but based on what YOU have told us, we have to make some changes to the way we do things.
