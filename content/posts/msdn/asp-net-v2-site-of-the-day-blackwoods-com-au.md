hey Frank,
I don't think blackwoods is really a great example. It may have been great for the developers, but the UI is seriously lacking form the perspectives of this here shopper.  It really feels like it was designed by a coder ;)  you know, everything sorted in a fixed heirarchy, one that probably matches the db schema.  But as a user, I want a site that lets me
SHOP
.
For example, the site is layed out as if I already know the exact product I want. That is, I have to navigate by brand, not product type.  If that's the case, then I'm probably shopping for a best price, but they don't list any.
But most of the time I know I want a certain product that does a certain job.  Brand maybe a final deciding factor, rarely if ever a first factor.  So I want to see drills for example, ALL BRANDS, then sort them by cordless or impact drills, or drill presses etc.  And I want to compare them, see why I should pay more for one drill over another.
That to me is shopping, and I do buy the kind of stuff blackwoods sells, but I sure don't want to go through the agony of trying to buy from their site.
