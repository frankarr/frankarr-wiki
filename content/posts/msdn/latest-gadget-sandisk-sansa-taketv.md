Latest Gadget : Sandisk Sansa TakeTV I picked up the 8gb version of SanDisk Sansa®TakeTV™ PC to TV Video Player and I must say I am impressed. It is a memory stick which has a little cradle that plugs into a tv. It has a remote control which pops out to drive the thing. There is a related service called Fanfare where you can purchase / download TV Shows. This thing just works.
