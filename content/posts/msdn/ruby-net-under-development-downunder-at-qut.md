Ruby.NET under development downunder at QUT I was catching up on my reading today and spotted the following post from Serial Imagine Cup Participant, Doug Stockwell: Ruby.NET has a web site, and a name? First mentioned on the Gardens Point Parser Generator site some time ago and largely unnoticed by the internet in general, Ruby.NET now has it's own page giving a bit more info, even an expected release schedule. This has already started a few waves. The main aim of the project is to support 100% of Ruby language semantics, but as I have posted about in the past this is not an easy problem.
