80-20 Retention Server for Microsoft SharePoint - no cost EDRM
I got an email from David Gillespie, who is the CTO for
80-20 Software
, a great local ISV focusing on document and records management technologies with a focus on corporate governance, risk and compliance (GRC) management.
Well, these guys have just released
80-20 Retention Server
, which is a
free
add on to Sharepoint Server which automates the retention process by applying information retention policies as part of everyday business processes. Go Aussie!!!
As David gave the background to me in the recent email
Frank,
I don't know if you caught the fuss in October over
this post from Ferris Research
that says SharePoint isn't ECM followed by a healthy defense from
Bil Simser
who compared it to the infamous 5 things wrong with sharepoint post ... but one of the things Ferris highlight was SharePoint's lack of records management (they compare it to the Oracle equivalent which includes RM in a later post).
I just wanted to let you know that we've fixed that little problem with
a server add-on to SharePoint
that does full-bodied enterprise Records Management and we are giving it away for free. Mark Harrison at MS UK has been helping us a lot with the beta and each time he has blogged about it there has been an enormous response (which suggests there might just be a need for this out there).
And for the developer's amongst us, the web part and asp.net application (presentation layer) are managed code.  In addition, there is a .Net assembly that both the web part and the asp.net application use, which uses COM Interop to plug back into the the rest of the system.
