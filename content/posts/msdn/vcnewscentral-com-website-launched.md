VCNewsCentral.com Website Launched Today (Another NickHaC Production) Howdy friends and email list buddies... I wanted to tell you about a News website i have just launched called https://www.VCNewsCentral.com The site features news and articles on starting, funding and managing new business ideas - including marketing, entrepreneurship, sales, innovation, technology and venture capital aswell as a bunch of other interesting stuff. The site is updated automatically every 30 minutes, 24 hours a day. The site lists all the leading industry news sources and then allows YOU to vote on it. By voting you tell other users on the site that the particular news item is interesting in some way. Votes make the system better for everyone, as the most popular news items float to the top. Let me know what you think of VCNewsCentral, and if you have any suggestions or feedback on how to improve the site it would be great to get more ideas on how to make VCNewsCentral better.
