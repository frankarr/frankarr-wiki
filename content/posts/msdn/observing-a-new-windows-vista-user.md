Observing a new Windows Vista User So, it's been a while since 'Rigo installed Windows Vista RC1 , and I have been intrigued watching him adapt. He used to have Google as his home page, Trillian as his IM client, Firefox as his browser. He now has Windows Live as his home page (customised with his favorite feeds), Windows Live Messenger for IM, Windows Live Toolbar, Windows Live Favorites, IE7 and has tweaked the sidebar.
