Yet Another Aussie .NET Blogger list Maintenance Day It's that time of the month (no, not THAT time) when I tidy up the Aussie .NET blogger list. I've got a few additions to make - Matt Dunn and new Readify hire Rocky Heckman Clarke Scott reckons I should remove folks from the list who havent been updating their blog . I'm reluctant to remove these bloggers, as they may just be busy with other stuff. As was the case with Matt Bourne recently.
