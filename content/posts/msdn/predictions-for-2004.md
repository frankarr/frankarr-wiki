Predictions for 2004 Graeme Philipson , an “old school” IT journo here in Australia has made a few predictions for 2004, which were recently published in the SMH .  They are a mixture of global (1,2,3,4,6,8,10) & local (5,7,9) predictions. In summary he makes the following prognostications: Sun Microsystems will be acquired. Dell will move closer to overtaking HP as number two vendor. Oracle's bid for Peoplesoft will succeed. Linux will arrive. Telstra will still be majority government owned. The volume of spam will continue to grow. "Offshoring" will become an enormous political issue. Recording companies will still not work out a way to stop free music downloads. The Federal Government will still not understand IT. IBM will still be number one.
