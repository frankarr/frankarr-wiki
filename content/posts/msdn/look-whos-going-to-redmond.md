Look who's going to Redmond You may not know this, but I have been feeling pretty sad recently. One of my team members is moving to a new role based in Microsoft Corp. I'm talking about Jason McConnell and he speaks more about the new role in this recent posting. I'm sad, because Jason was the first person I hired into the role. I liked him, and will miss him, alot. It kinda like seeing my son have his first day at school , except it's more like boarding school, really far away. Now, what this also does is present a new opportunity. A chance to meet someone new. Someone to step into Jason's sizeable shoes. Someone to fill that gap. Someone to continue doing great work. We have just posted the job description on the Microsoft Careers site . The position is based in Melbourne. Don't get me wrong, I'm also really happy for Jason . This is a great move for him and I know he will do well. So, come along to the Security Summit and say goodbye to Jason.
