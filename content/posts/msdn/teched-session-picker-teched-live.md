TechEd Session Picker : TechEd Live The bar has been well and truly raised. Craig Bailey has posted an awesome "app" which consists of two parts - an online experience and a downloadable application. The online app, developed by Scott Scovell, has a very nice Web 2.0 like web site for setting your TechEd sessions, sharing them with your friends, then choosing and viewing your friends on the maps. And the best part, it's built with .Net 2.0, SQL and Atlas. The site is at www.talmanlive.com When you register you can import your vCalendar file (eg from Bill McCarthy's app or Paul Stovell's WPF app ) and you can upload your meego too. Nice work guys!
