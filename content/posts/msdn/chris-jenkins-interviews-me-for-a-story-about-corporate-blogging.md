Chris Jenkins interviews me for a story about Corporate Blogging I had a interview with Chris Jenkins , the online editor Australian IT , today. He is doing a story on Corporate Blogging in Australia, and I was fortunate to be one of his interview candidates. As I told Chris, blogs give Microsoft another excellent way to communicate with our communities and if employees understand how to "blog smart", then common business blogging fears can be overcome. I've done a few of these interviews in the past, ranging from The Bulletin over 12 months ago ( Josh you were ahead of the curve!), Computerworld back in November , and most recently with Business Sunday. Looking forward to seeing this story come out soon.
