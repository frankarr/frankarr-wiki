VSIP Booth at Teched Australia & Beanie At TechEd Australia , we will be raising the awareness of our Visual Studio Industry Partner Program (VSIP) partners We have 7 VSIP's signed up and will take 4 booths where the VSIP's will show their tools' VS Integration capabilities.  The VSIP pavilion will take up booths 15, 16, 17 and 18 and will be highlighted by a “red carpet” and a large banner over the booths. Our Sponsors signed up are: Borland, HP, Serena, Business Objects, Amberpoint, Compuware & Microfocus. The VSIP Pavilion is also the “proud sponsor” of the “Ask the Experts Dinner” where there will be ample signage and 50 wait staff will wear beanies with the VSIP logo as well as the “Score some Recognition” tee shirts. This will be a little “loyalty card” which will be given out at the “Experts Dinner”.  Delegates will need to get a stamp from 5 of the 7 VSIP's (each VSIP will have a different rubber stamp) to claim their “Winter Warmer” which is a blue or red beanie with the VSIP branding on it.  They will then take the card to the MSDN stand to claim their beanie. Offer is limited to the first 1,000 completed cards and expires at the end of the conference. So, come over to the pavillion, meet the VSIP folks, and score a beanie. It will be cold in Canberra, so this will be handy.
