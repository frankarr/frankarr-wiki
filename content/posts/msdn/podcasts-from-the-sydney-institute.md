Podcasts from The Sydney Institute I have been involved in a little side project which kicked off recently. Some folks within Microsoft come and asked me to give them some help in getting podcasts produced. I took them through the range of possibilities, from home grown productions to outsourcing to third parties. The result of all of this is online now at The Sydney Institute Podcast The Sydney Institute is Australia's premier current affairs forum. Every week contemporary political, social, cultural and economic issues are explored at the Institute through dialogue and discussion. The Sydney Institute Podcast delivers select forum discussions in MP3 audio format for use on your computer or personal digital music player. The first podcast is with The Hon Malcolm Turnbull MP, on water management.
