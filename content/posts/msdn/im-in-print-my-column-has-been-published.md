I'm in print - My column has been published A while back I wrote a post about my contribution for a local tech enthusiasts magazine and I wondered what I would write about. Well, the magazine has gone to print and my page is in it! The magazine is WindowsXP Magazine , published by Derwent Howard , and it is the November 2006 edition. The magazine has a forum , but none of the articles are online. The section is at the back of the magazine and it's titled "This digital life", which features a nice sof focus photo of me, with the article I wrote, and the about page from my blog (which reminds me, I need to update that page) So,what did I write about??? I could say " Buy The Magazine " But I won't. I wrote about the thing I am most passionate about - my kids and their adventures with technology.
