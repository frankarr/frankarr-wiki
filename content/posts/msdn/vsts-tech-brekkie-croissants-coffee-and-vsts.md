VSTS Tech Brekkie - Croissants, Coffee and VSTS
Now, this is a cool idea.
VSTS Tech Brekkie's
are a series of free breakfast presentations on topics relating to Microsoft Visual Studio Team System. The events are run by the local developer community with the support of Microsoft, local businesses and speakers who contribute to the overall success of the series.
Brekkie dates:
Friday 6th October 2006
Friday 13th October 2006
Friday 20th October 2006
Friday 27th October 2006
The current VSTS Tech Brekkie series is running in
