My Dream App You may not know this , but I am a passionate AFL fan . I also love my XDA II. The dream is to combine these two passions. I have a vision of an app (ideally .net compact framework based ) that i have on my XDA II which does the following: provides realtime update of score from the current afl game provides historical stats of past games grabs news about my team and makes them available for me to read at my leisure provide some overall stats- league ladder , leading goalkickers, etc It's a great smart client app that has all the data I want at my fingertips, but updated as appropriate Now, who is going to do this for me?
