Australian .NET Developer mailing list Dr Peter Stanski has asked me to spread the word about the Australian .NET mailing list. Here's the scoop: To Subscribe to the list, send email to: imailsrv@stanski.com In body of message type: subscribe dotnet To Unsubscribe from list, send email to: imailsrv@stanski.com In body of message type: unsubscribe dotnet Send your email messages to list via: dotnet@stanski.com Incoming Emails have a Subject line prefix: [aus-dotnet] Use Email Client rules to file into folders or use exchange rules Enjoy!
