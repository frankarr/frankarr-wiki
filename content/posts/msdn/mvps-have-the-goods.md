MVPs have the goods Further to my earlier post about new MVPs , let me point you to a lovely story from one of our local Aussie MVPs, Darren Neimke . Turns out Darren had some free copies of VSTS to give out , and he then made a local developer very very happy . That's what MVPs can do. Darren calls out for a small clearing house, so I am going to step in and lend a hand. Leave a comment, or go stalk an MVP, tell us why you should get a free copy of VSTS. No guarantees, but I will make sure that any comments left to me will be passed onto local MVPs.
