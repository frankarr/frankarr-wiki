Judging the Queensland Innovation Centre Vista Gadget Competition Today I am performing my judging duties for the Queensland Innovation Centre Vista Gadget Competition , which has been run by Marty Gnanananthan. I have 5 gadgets to evaluate South East Queensland Dam Levels Casket Sidebar Gadget Queensland Weather Queensland Traffic SurfWatch 1.0 The criteria I have been asked to consider is Originality Creativity Innovation I have featured some of these gadgets on my blog recently, but I can't let that sway my decision. Wish me luck!
