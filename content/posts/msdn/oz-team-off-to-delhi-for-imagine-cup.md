Oz team off to Delhi for Imagine Cup Nice to see our Imagine Cup Winners getting some coverage. Oz team off to Delhi for Imagine Cup Indian Link, 22/07/06 This article reports that a team of Indian students from the University of Canberra won second place in Microsoft' s Imagine Cup. The team was recognised for their Ambulance Service Management System (ASMS). Microsoft Australia manager, Ben English, states that the ASMS is a great example of how the IT industry and the tertiary sector can collaborate to foster innovation in the healthcare sector.
