Global Corporate Challenge : 100 Days of Walking A big milestone today. Not only is it the first day of Spring, but it's also the 100th day of the Global Corporate Challenge . 100 days of walking and we have reached Checkpoint 4, Stage 77. So, last time I checked our overall team status was over a month ago . Things weren't going well back then. How have we progressed? Well, on the official standings, we're placed 1005th , our slide continues. Looking at the team stats, our average continues to hover around the 9000 step mark. We are below the average of the overall GCC. I am happy to see that I have crossed the 2,000,000 mark. In fact, during the week of TechEd, I covered 141,746 steps Much to my chagrin, we continue to chase Readify 3361 . On the plus side, we're doing pretty well amongst our other challengers. But what really concerns/annoys me is that we are missing over 90 step entries . In fact, we're missing 92 entries . At an average of 9000 steps, thats at least 828,000 extra steps we could have in the total! So, who is letting the team down? Hmmm.  Next time you see Dave Lemphers or Jeff Alexander , ask them how their steps are going.
