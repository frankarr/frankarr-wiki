AASG : MOSSIG Updater Vista Sidebar Gadget Nice to see more Sidebar gadgets appearing. My latest discovery is the MOSSIG Updater Vista Sidebar Gadget MOSSIG is the Microsoft Office System Special Interest Group (MOSSIG) and they meet on the 4th Wednesday of every month in Melbourne and Sydney.
