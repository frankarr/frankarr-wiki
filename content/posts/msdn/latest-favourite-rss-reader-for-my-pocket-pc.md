Latest Favourite RSS Reader for my Pocket PC I raved about RSS Sync a few months ago, which is an RSS reader I am using on my XDA II. Dave Glover introduced me to another one a few weeks back - an app called Egress. Egress is a application which can display your favorite RSS feeds directly on your PocketPC. Features Supports RSS (.09, .91, .92 and 2.0), RDF (1.0), and ATOM (0.3) Supports landscape orientation using PocketPC 2003SE Integrates with Today Caches and displays inline images Background download of feeds Supports HTTP proxies Configurable item retention Configurable news feed storage location Configurable fonts and colors Supports alternate web browsers Supports Radio Userland style automatic subscription (Web page coffee mug icon) I've been using a few weeks and I really really like it. This is now my RSS reader of choice on my XDA II. Comments Anonymous August 10, 2004 Thanks!  I just got a pocket pc and I was looking for a good reader for it.  I'll try this out now!
