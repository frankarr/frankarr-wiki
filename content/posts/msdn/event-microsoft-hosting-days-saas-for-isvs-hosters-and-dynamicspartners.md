Event: Microsoft Hosting Days - SaaS for ISVs, Hosters and DynamicsPartners Sydney – Tuesday 13 February 2007, Microsoft Sydney Melbourne – Wednesday 21 February 2007, Microsoft Melbourne Brisbane – Tuesday 27 February 2007, Microsoft Brisbane Register now for the Microsoft Hosting Day and learn how to capitalize on the growing demand for software-as-a-service (SaaS). This full day seminar will give you the knowledge and tools you need to expand your business whether you are a Pure Hoster, Telco, Independent Software Vendor (ISV), Reseller (VAR), System Integrator or Microsoft Dynamics Partner. The Microsoft Hosting Day is an annual event that offers a great opportunity to meet Microsoft professionals and partners of the hosting ecosystem. This FREE seminar will cover: • The latest news about the growing opportunities for SaaS on Windows technology • A wide range of business opportunities available with Microsoft Hosting solutions, including Windows-based Hosting for Applications, Hosted Messaging and Collaboration, Hosted Dynamics and Hosted CRM • Aligning your business with Microsoft’s product roadmap – so you can maximize benefits from the planned testing, release and availability of Microsoft products and services, especially with SharePoint 3.0 and IIS 7.0 • Using best practices to sell SaaS offers or business-class e-mail to small and medium-sized businesses. The more services you provide, the more customers you will attract and retain. Windows-based hosting allows you to create value-added offerings and this seminar will tell you how. For ISVs and Microsoft Dynamics Partners offering or planning to offer software-as-a-service (SaaS), we will cover the following: • How to take advantage of the Microsoft Service Provider License Agreement (SPLA) • How to capitalize on the growing demand for SaaS • The latest news about opportunities for SaaS on Windows • SaaS offers that will help you grow business opportunities Places are limited, so register for the Microsoft Hosting Day today! We look forward to seeing you there. Sincerely, The Microsoft Hosting Team microsoft.com/hosting © 2007 Microsoft Corporation. All Rights Reserved.
