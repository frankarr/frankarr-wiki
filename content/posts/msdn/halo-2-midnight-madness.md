Halo 2 Midnight Madness I'm heading out into Sydney city to help out on the Halo2 Midnight Madness . This is happening at Myer in the Pitt Street Mall . There is also an event in Melbourne, in the Bourke St Mall outside Myer. At midnight , the doors will open and excited XBOX gamers will be able to purchase Halo2 - before our brothers & sisters on the other side of the world. There will be bands, food and lots of fun & games. I am manning one of the demo units, so you can even get to play the game! So, if you are in Sydney or Melbourne , head down and be part of the madness!
