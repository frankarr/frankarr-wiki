Is Gnoos, Is Good I scored an invite to the Beta version of Gnoos - an Aussie Blog Search engine, and one of the few Aussie Web 2.0 ventures, headed up by Ben Barren . And as a good Web 2.0 company, they have a blog to accompany the beta I've had a quick play and I like what I see - top searchs, hot tags, and some global categories such as Aussie Blogs, Aussie Media and Global Blogs. Of course I had to do vanity search. Isn't that the first thing we do?? There's some other neat features in the result set, but I'll keep that quiet for the time being :) Good work gnoos team.
