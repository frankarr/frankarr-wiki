I want to attend the Blogger's Lunch Folks have asked me how do they register for the Blogger's Lunch. Follow these simple steps, and you can't go wrong. Log onto Commnet Select the My Profile menu item under MeetingPoint Home menu option Check "I want to attend the Blogger's Lunch" Don't forget to add your blog url It should look something like this (granted this is the speaker view, so what you see may be a bit different) I want to see EVERYONE there!
