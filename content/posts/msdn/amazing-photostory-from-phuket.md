Amazing Photostory from Phuket Will Poole , Senior Vice President for Microsoft's Windows Client Business, was in Phuket with his family when the Tsunami hit. He captured a lot of before and after photos and created a first hand Photo Story (this is a 6mb file, I recommend right-clicking on the link and doing a Save Target As). Oh, You will also need Windows Media Player 10 or Photo Story 3 to watch this.
