iDog comes to keep me company Deeps got back this week following his trip to Japan for the Imagine Cup. He brought me back a present - an iDog. Now, you may ask - what is an iDog ?? Well, The iDog connects to PCs or MP3 players via an audio jack and can be use as a speaker, but it also has sensors to react to users by moving its ears and light up LEDs. One kind soul has even translated the Japanese instruction manual to english. The iDog even has its own site , but it would help if you can read Japanese. Check out the movie on the site, it's a classic! Let me tell you, that it's the cutest little gadget I've seen for awhile, even better than my USB Aquarium. I had to post a photo of the little fellow . Awww, isn't he cute. Hopefully, his fate will be better than my previous puppy, LuLu! Maybe I'll bring him to TechEd on the Gold Coast with me.....
