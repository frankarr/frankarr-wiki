Finding People's Blogs In the lift today at work, a collegue asked me how to find people's blogs. He told me he would go to a search engine and type in the name of the person but he couldn't find them. Now, the topic of blog discovery is well known and frequently discussed , and the supply of blog search engines is plentiful. I wasn't going to bore him with meta directory blah blah blah. Plus, it was only a short lift ride. I ask if he was looking for an Australian blogger . He said yes. I told him to just go to Gnoos. He tells me that he found the person he was looking for.
