STIRR Sydney Now this is interesting. I spotted this on Yoick and then on Martin Well's blog STIRR is a casual event for people who work in Internet start ups. It's brought to you by the STIRR Network and our sponsors: Tech Venture Partners, Atlassian and Tangler . It's a fun way to meet some new people and laugh about life online. Grab a drink, grab a fancy party pie and chat. This is our first one here, so there is a little bit of 'how do you think it should go' and a little bit of 'let's see how it goes'. So, when and where is this little shindig: Hippos @ 620 Harris Street, Ultimo ( Local Live Map ) Wednesday 6th December 2006, 7pm More Info at the STIRR Sydney Wiki Sounds intriguing.
