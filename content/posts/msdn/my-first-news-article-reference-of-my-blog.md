My first news article reference of my blog... Yeah, I know that's a weird subject line, but bear with me...... Today there was a story in the mainstream media - Computerworld - that referenced a post from my blog . It was in relation to a local ISV, CDT ,  who has developed a application called FileSphere . I wrote up a rather excited post after having a demo of the application. I was quoted, or rather, my post was quoted. Wow! The phenomenon mentioned by a recent businessweek story is being repeated all over the world. Now that's cool. And by the way, Filesphere really is cool.
