ActiveSync Over Bluetooth between M200 and K-Jam : It Works! One really exciting side benefit of the AKU2 Update has been seeing ActiveSync over Bluetooth actually work! My M200 runs a bunch of Toshiba utilities, so I couldn't follow the guidelines posted on club iimate- i-mateâ¢ K-JAM Bluetooth ActiveSync Setup (PDF). So, I made up my own steps: I activated Bluetooth on the M200 and made it discoverable I used the Bluetooth Local COM applet to delete *ALL* the virtual COM ports. I then created a new virtual COM Port on COM6 . This was of type LocalCOM-Server , which means it will handle incoming requests from a device. I then set ActiveSync on my M200 to use COM6 , via the connection settings. Then, I went to my K-Jam, turned on Bluetooth, made it discoverable and created a New partnership The K-Jam detected my M200 and I was able to connect to it Then something magical happened. The ActiveSync service was detected. I then went to ActiveSync on the K-Jam and selected "Connect via Bluetooth" It connected and synched! It really synched. I'm still in shock that it worked.
