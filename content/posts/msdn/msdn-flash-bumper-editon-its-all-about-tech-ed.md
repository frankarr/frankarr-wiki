MSDN Flash Bumper Editon : It's all about Tech.Ed The next edition of the MSDN Flash is due real soon now. It's a bumber edition, revealing lots of info about Tech.Ed 2005 , including the special rates for MSDN Connection member or MSDN Subscriptions ($600 off!!) , but I wanted to hilight some of the social activities. Welcome Party: Powered by Xbox - Tuesday 30 August Soak in the Xbox buzz at the official Tech.Ed Welcome Party. This is your chance to mingle with the exhibitors, rub shoulders with Xbox characters, watch a show or demo and race a car. Take on Dean Loaney, Australian Halo 2 champ , in the ultimate Frag Fest. I played Dean this week and he whipped my butt. Experts Forum - Wednesday 31 August A chance to speak with our keynote speakers one-on-one? Meet with industry experts, exhibitors and other notable personalities at the Tech.Ed Experts Forum. Come and mingle socially, or start a debate with an expert in a specific field, as you enjoy drinks and a bite to eat. Tech.Ed 2005 Party - Thursday 1 September A night out at Warner Bros Movie World . A BIG night of food, drinks, rides and live entertainment awaits.
