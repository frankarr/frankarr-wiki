Tech.Ed Student Day : August 22nd For the past few years, we have run a Student Day during Tech.Ed. We've held this on the day before Tech.Ed opens, and it has been a well attended gig. This year, our intern Berno has been working hard to make it a great event. A draft of the agenda has been published , and I'm on the card. This year I will be covering Office 2007 and it's relevance to students. I'm going to have a helper, a young student by the name of Long Zheng. Remember him? He was the young man who created a bunch of screencast featuring Windows Vista and Office 2007 and he got world wide attention. Anyways, it's happening August 22nd, more info here and registration here . Should be fun.
