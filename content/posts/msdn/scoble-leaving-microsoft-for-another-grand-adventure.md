Scoble leaving Microsoft for another grand adventure
The blogosphere rumor mill has been
buzzing with the news that
Robert Scoble is leaving Microsoft
, with
Chris
Pirillo the most adamant
.
Robert then posted his response,
Correcting
the Record about Microsoft
.
For me, this is both sad news and happy news.
Sad, because Microsoft is losing a loud cheerleader
Happy, because I think Robert has done what he can at Micosoft and he is
ready for the next phase of his life, to be closer to his son, to slow down and
to enjoy the journey.  I remember
catching up
with Robert earlier this year
, and he was looking tired and worn out. I'm
sure this change will be good for him and his family.
I've been honored to have been associated with
Robert
over the years.
Just
this weekend, Trevor Cook mentioned us together in a recent post
.
Anyways, time to ask some questions and try to answer them too.
Will a new Microsoft Uber blogger emerge? Maybe. Does it matter?
There's over 3,000 MSFT folks blogging now
Will I still be reading
Robert's blog
? Yup
Will I be leaving too (with
Cam
credited for starting that rumour
) ? Ah, no. No one else would hire
me.
Robert - All the best with the new venture.
