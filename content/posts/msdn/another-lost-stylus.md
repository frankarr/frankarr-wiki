I've lost two from my O2 XDA IIs thanks to having a lack of sticky-tape on the sides, combined with using the belt-clip. Now, I keep it in my pocket, but I still managed to lose it on Monday. Yesterday I got it back, thanks to Mariel at work, who found it. This one I didn't want to lose, as it is also a pen. :)
In case you're not doing it already, use magic tape, and put it on an angle. That way, the thickness increases and decreases slowly as you go up the stylus, and the magic tape is less slippery than normal sticky tape. By putting it on at an angle, you can go a bit thicker than otherwise, so it can be more effective.
