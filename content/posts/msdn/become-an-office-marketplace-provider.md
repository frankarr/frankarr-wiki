Become an Office Marketplace provider
If your product or service works with Microsoft Office it should be a part of the Microsoft Office Marketplace.
The Office Marketplace is part of Microsoft Office Online, the redesigned Office Web site that serves as the online companion to the Microsoft Office System.  On this site, Office users can find a directory of tools, services, smart tags, and add-ins offered by external providers that allow them to do more with Office.  Office Online Australia will launch in the first half of 2004.  Please visit
https://www.microsoft.com/australia/office/online/
to find out more and to register your interest.
