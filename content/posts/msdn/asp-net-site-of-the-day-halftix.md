ASP.NET Site of the Day -- Halftix Local Sydney institution Halftix has an ASP.NET site developed by the folks at Readify HalfTix was established in 1987 as a half-price/discounted ticketing service by The Australian Elizabethan Theatre Trust. The HalfTix concept was born from the desire to offer people low-cost access to live entertainment and to help entertainers reach the largest possible audience. Like many discount box offices worldwide, HalfTix positioned itself in the middle of the city. For 12 years, it provided a friendly, well-respected discount ticketing outlet at Martin Place. Renovations to the CBD meant the booth had to go. Today HalfTix is privately owned by a group of five visionaries, who are committed to ensuring that this essential and popular service continues. With the benefit of technology, HalfTix now offers online ticket purchasing, as well as a national ticketing service. The first new shop front located at 91 York Street (in the Council Exchanges CIEE building) opened in August 2002, with further outlets to open in late 2002.
