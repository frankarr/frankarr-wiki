In 2 weeks the Global Corporate Challenge Begins!
My team and I have signed up for the
Global Corporate Challenge
. It started as a challenge from the boys from
Readify
, and well, we're all up for a
challenge!
I recently received the Welcome Pack and have distributed it to the team
members -
Andrew
,
Chuck
,
David
,
Dave
,
Jeff
,
Michael
.
What is this all about?? Well, we get to wear an official GCC Pedometer for 6
months. Each day we record in the steps we walked into the
GCC website
, and this plots how far we
have walked around the world.
Now, the
GCC
is not a race. But
since we were challenged by those
Readifyians
,
we're going to at least be competitive. As the team captain, I have asked each
member to average
10,000
steps a day.  A big ask, but it
can only lead to good things.
So, this officially kicks off May 25. This gives us 2 weeks to get used to
our new friend the GCC Pedometer.
Me - I'm going to start taking the stairs from the carpark each day. That
will be good for a 1000 steps or so.
BTW - The
Global Corporate Challenge
website
is a huge Flash application, sitting on a server running
IIS6
. It's an award winnning site, by award
winning web development company
Reative Media
