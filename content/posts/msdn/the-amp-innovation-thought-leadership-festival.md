The AMP Innovation & Thought Leadership Festival I have been invited to speak at the upcoming AMP Innovation & Thought Leadership Festival. It's an event run by AMP for AMP staff. The ring leader is Annalie Killian who has the title of "Catalyst for Magic”. That's almost as cool as Nick Hodge's various identities. I am going to cover my favourite topic - AFL Football. No, actually I am going to talk about Blogging (seriously, what else do I do these days?) Des Walsh is also involved, running a series of workshops for the staff . Last time I hooked up with Des was at the "Blogs, Wikis & RSS conference". Good to see a large Australia corporation having a serious look at blogging and social media. (Don't forget the free introductory guide to social media ) My contact at AMP tells me that 54 people have already confirmed for my session - let's see if i can inspire 10% of them to get blogging!
