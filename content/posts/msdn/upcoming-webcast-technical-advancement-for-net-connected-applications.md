Technical Advancement for .NET Connected Applications
Integration of applications is one of the hottest topics in IT today. It is driving your customers' interest in Web services and service-oriented architecture. IDC predicts the market for Web services will reach US$30 billion by 2010. Join this hour-long webcast, and see how Microsoft can help ISVs to get ready for this huge opportunity.
