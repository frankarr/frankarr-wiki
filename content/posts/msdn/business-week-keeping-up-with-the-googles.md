Business Week : Keeping Up with the Googles Here's something I never thought I would see, a semi positive article in Business Week Keeping Up with the Googles The delay of Microsoft Corp.'s Windows Vista operating system until next year makes the software giant look like a dinosaur stuck in the tar, but that's not the whole picture. Its efforts to match the Googles of the world with fast online innovations seem to be paying off. In the five months since Microsoft (MSFT) announced its Windows Live strategy, it has released no fewer than 20 new services that are attracting millions of consumers. "They now gain some of that agility that Google (GOOG) has enjoyed," says analyst Van Baker of researcher Gartner Inc.
