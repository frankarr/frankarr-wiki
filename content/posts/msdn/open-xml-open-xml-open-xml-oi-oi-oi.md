Open XML, Open XML, Open XML, Oi, Oi, Oi !!!! Ok, so it doesn't work as a war cry This year is an important year for Microsoft and its Partners as Ecma Office Open XML, the document format for Office 2007, is currently being considered for ratification as an international standard before the International Organization for Standardization (ISO). Some of the benefits of Open XML include: Compatibility with billions of existing documents Serves users needs in the areas of cultural diversity, accessibility and the long-term preservation of documents Puts control over the evolution of the standards in the hands of the largest user community (i.e. the ISO) Learn more and share your support for the adoption of Open XML as an ISO standard That war cry again - Open XML, Open XML, Open XML, Oi, Oi, Oi !!!!!
