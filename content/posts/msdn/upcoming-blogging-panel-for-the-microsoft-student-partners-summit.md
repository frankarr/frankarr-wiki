Upcoming Blogging Panel for the Microsoft Student Partners Summit I’ve been asked to pull together a panel on Blogging @ Microsoft for the upcoming Microsoft Student Partners (MSP) Summit. The vision is to have a panel where the MSPs can heard from key Microsoft bloggers and understand if the MSPs can make a career out of blogging (kinda like my friend Long Zheng has been able to do), or how can they use this as a tool, get tips, sources and how can they improve their blog. I’m going to be the host and I’ve reached out to some potential panelists – so I’ll keep you posted. Should be a lot of fun.
