Windows Embedded Family I've been speaking to a bunch of folks recently about developing embedded solutions . It's quite amazing how much of this is happening in the market. It is really under the radar of the core business, but it is a major growth area. Now, there is confusion as to what options developers have. It really boils down to these two platforms: Windows CE .NET the operating system that is powering the next generation of smart, connected, small-footprint devices. Windows XP Embedded , enables rapid development of the most reliable and full-featured connected devices. Which to Choose?
