Hello to my Moblog I finally get around to setting up a moblog. I'm using a free service from textamerica . It's really easy to setup and use. Lots of predefined templates and layouts. Very slick. And here's the connection - it is an ASP.NET site! To use, it's pretty simple. I take a photo with my XDA II and then use the builtin MMS application to send the image to my account at textamerica.
