ISV Buddy Program We're opening up the ISV Buddy program for ISVs in Australia & New Zealand. If you are in ISV, head over the ISV Community center and register your interest. You may get a developer from the Visual Studio team as your buddy, or you could even get me or one of the local folks who work at Microsoft. If you are developing a software product or solution on a Microsoft platform you are eligible to participate.
