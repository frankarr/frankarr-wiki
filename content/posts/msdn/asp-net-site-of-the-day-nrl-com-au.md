ASP.NET Site of the Day - NRL.COM.AU The NRL season kicks off tonight, and I thought I would let you know that the Official NRL site is an ASP.NET site, built using DNN . (But not ASP.NET v2) Some of you may know that I am an one-eyed AFL fan (and my team is St Kilda ). but on this occassion I will make an exception and talk about the NRL. When I lived in Canberra, I was a pseudo Raider's supporter , but now, I don't know which team to really care about. I have no real affinity with any of these teams - Broncos, Bulldogs, Raiders, Cowboys, Sea Eagles, Storm, Knights, Eels, Panthers, Sharks, Rabbitohs, Dragons, Roosters, Warriors, Wests Tigers or Titans Who should I support? Should I continue to not care? What a dilema. [ Currently Playing : Cry! Cry!
