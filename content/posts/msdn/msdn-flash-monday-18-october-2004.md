Anna Uncovers the Foundations for Successful Architecture
Hi, everyone. I'm Anna Liu, and I'm an architect adviser based at Microsoft Australia, Sydney. I am part of the Architecture team here, working with architects in large businesses and government agencies, evangelising architecture best practices and proven design patterns. Our greatest passion is to help those with an architectural responsibility to build high-performing, highly scalable, highly secure enterprise-strength systems that may or may not span across heterogeneous platforms.Here's a quick look at some of this week's essential references:
If you haven't done so, now is as good a time as any to visit the MSDN Architecture Resource Centre. You can subscribe to the Architects' Journal and find lots of valuable resources on topics such as Service-Oriented Architecture, Web Services, integration and interoperability.
At Patterns and Practices, you can find some excellent guidance for building enterprise-strength applications and systems. Whether you're an application developer, architect, or an IT Professional in charge of operations and management, you'll find some gems of experience that might just solve your most pressing workplace problem.
Hope you enjoy the rest of the issue, and happy building!
Critical Security Alerts
Create Web apps with Visual Web Developer 2005 Express and SQL Server Express. Part 1 of a three-part series covers setting up a project and a SQL Server Express database, and explores the use of the ASP.NET Web Site Administration tool to set up roles, users, and providers. It also covers security controls such as Login, LoginStatus, LoginView, CreateUserWizard, PasswordRecovery, and ChangePassword.
JPEGs can carry a lot more than just a pretty picture. Newly discovered issues in JPEG processing technology have been found to affect a range of software supporting this image format. The good news is that a simple, free solution is available in the form of a security update.
