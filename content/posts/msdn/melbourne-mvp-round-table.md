Melbourne MVP Round table I was able to attend a recent roundtable with some Melbourne based MVPs - Ben Walters, Bill Chesnut, Bill McCarthy, Glenn Wilson , Greg Linwood, Henry Craven, Kevin Francis, Dr Peter Stanski, Shauna Kelly & Slav Pidgorny. Just like we did in Sydney , we ate some food, introduced ourselves, cracked some jokes, and had some good discussions - especially around INETA and CULMINUS , and how to make them real in this part of the world. One excellent outcome was having some of the local Microsoft Melbourne folks come along to the meeting, to meet the local MVPs. This doesn't happen often enough. Thanks to all who came along.
