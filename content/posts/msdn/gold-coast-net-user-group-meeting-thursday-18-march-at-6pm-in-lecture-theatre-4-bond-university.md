Gold Coast .NET User Group Meeting Thursday, 18 March, at 6PM in Lecture Theatre 4, Bond University.
Congratulations to the
Gold Coast .NET User Group
and best wishes on their first meeting on Thursday, 18 March, at 6PM.  Here's hoping you fill Lecture Theatre 4!!!
The time has come for the Gold Coast to have its own .NET User Group! The first meeting will be held on Thursday, 18 March, at 6PM in Lecture Theatre 4, University Centre Building, Bond University. See
Map
for directions to Bond. We are extremely fortunate to have
Greg Low
as our first speaker.
Greg
is the president of the
Queensland .NET User Group
. He has just gotten back from a week of extensive training in Amsterdam on Microsoft's new "
Smart Client
" initiative.
Greg
will cover using the recently-released
Visual Studio Tools for Office
and building
Office Research Web Services
. Join
Greg
and be among the first to see this new technology, designed by Microsoft technology experts, delivered by one of the best industry-leading trainers in Australia. Everyone is invited! [
