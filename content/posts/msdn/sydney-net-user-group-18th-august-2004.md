Sydney .NET User Group 18th August, 2004 I got a reminder that the next Sydney .NET User Group is on18th August 2004. This month Adam will be covering the new Microsoft Access Migration Kit. He will also discuss related Win Form considerations for Access developers. Scott McCulloch will be following up on the structured text suggestion by Mehmet. Scott will also show you how the Provider Model works with DotNetNuke. Tatham will be continuing the series on the .NET Class Library Design Guidelines. He will focus on correct use of exceptions in the .NET Framework including best practices regarding custom exceptions and re-throwing. Wednesday 18th August. 6:00 pm at Microsoft, 1 Epping Road North Ryde See You There!! | Sorry I can't make it
