Atlanta NOT Atlantis I was preparing to head out on my trip to Atlanta which is where Microsoft is holding it's annual sales and technical conference, which we call MGB, when my daughter started to pout and look all despondant. She was angry with me for not taking her to Atlantis , where people live under the sea . I agree that this place makes us all feel wet, but that's due to the heat not because we live under the sea!
