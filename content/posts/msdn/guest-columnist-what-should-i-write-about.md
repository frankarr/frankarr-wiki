Guest Columnist : What Should I Write About ?? I've been asked to do a regular column (actually more like a page) for a local tech enthusiasts magazine. I feel honored to have been asked, but now I have to face up to writing something for a fast approaching deadline. I've been given a blank sheet of paper and it's all up to me, but to be honest. I'm struggling with the angle I should take. Since it's print, I have a long lead time and the timing of the next edition is September. September! I can't think beyond what I am going to have for dinner tonight. Sheeesh. Here's some of my ideas My recollections of the industry over the past 20 years, with a particular emphasis on the last 10 Random samples of ASCII Art that I have collected over the years A deconstruction of the current broadband situation in Australia An Insider view into Microsoft Australia - What makes it tick. Stories about my kids and their adventures with technology Vista How Tos Geek Jokes Are any of these interesting? What would you like to read?
