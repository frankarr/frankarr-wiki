Bank to lead in Vista uptake Spotted this in Australian IT today Bank to lead in Vista uptake WESTPAC will test Microsoft's Windows system Vista in December, in preparation for a launch of the platform to about 20,000 desktops across the bank. Westpac bank will be one of the first large corporates in Australia to upgrade to the upgraded operating system. So, it's not just 'Rigo that likes Windows Vista.
