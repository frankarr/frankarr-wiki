A Keyboard In The Mail for Frank I went to my mailslot today to collect my mail. There was a HUGE parcel in it. It was addressed to "Frank". Hmmmm - present? I opened it up and it was a keyboard. Weird. There was a note which included a phone number. I called the number and asked the guy why he had sent me a keyboard. Apparently he was talking to "Frank" at Microsoft earlier in the week and "Frank" told him to send it to him. Now, I wasn't that "Frank". But I've now got a busted keyboard which I need to get fixed for some guy. Time to hit the address book and work out who is the right "Frank".
