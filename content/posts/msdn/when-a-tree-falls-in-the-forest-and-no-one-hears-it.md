In desperation he turned to me. So, what am I going to do? Well, I've written this post. I'm going to ping the IE Blog team , link back to all the IE team members, Ali Alvi, Jeff Davis, Jeremy Epling, Bruce Morgan, Justin Rogers, Tony Schreiner and Chris Wilson and let the blogosphere do the rest. Tatham - Here's hoping your problem hasn't fallen on dead ears.
