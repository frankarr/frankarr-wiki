If you're in SA and want to get the good stuff, get along From: Peter Griffith Sent: Monday, 2 July 2007 1:48 PM To: Frank Arrigo Subject: CodeCampSA 7th-8th July programme Hi Frank, CodeCampSA is on this weekend 7 th -8 th July at UniSA, Theatre HH4-08 Hans Heysen Building, North Terrace, ADELAIDE SA Hans Heysen Building is closest to Register St off Hindley St (near BBQ Inn) 8.30 for 8.45 start. The vison of CodeCampSA is to bring to you a significant CodeCamp event for which you would normally have to travel interstate. See the outstanding programme below. We have assembled 15 presenters who are experts in their fields to speak to you over the weekend. That is the equivalent of over 12 months attendance at normal ADNUG meetings. A bonus is that attendance is FREE thanks to sponsorship of UniSA, ADNUG, ACS and K2.net. A number of these presenters are traveling from interstate to share with you their knowledge and experience. They are people not usually seen in Adelaide. You will have the opportunity to network with these people while they are here. Whilst aimed primarily at the Dot Net world, there are presentations that transcend these boundaries and are applicable to software development generally. Check out the detailed program for events of interest to you.
