The 2006 Census Tonight is Census Night! That one day every 5 years in when citizens answer questions for the goverment I wanted to complete the eCensus , but I was over-ruled by my wife who likes it 'old school'. So, Moira, the kids and I sat around the table and we filled in the form. Most of the questions were fine and I tried really hard to not muck around with my answers. We don't want another Jedi Incident However, I couldn't resist the following. It's all true.
