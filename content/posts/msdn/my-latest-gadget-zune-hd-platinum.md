My latest gadget – Zune HD Platinum Yes, I'm one of the ones who pre-ordered a Zune HD and got it delivered on the day of release. It's thin. It's light. It syncs fast. It has a browser. It has installable apps. The desktop client software is pretty cool too – a bunch of improvements and a bunch of new features So yes, I'm a pretty happy Zune user so far. All it's missing is a radio stack and it would be a perfect converged device.  Looking forward to seeing what the team is cooking up next! My only problem is wrestling with my kids to use it. But don't take my word for it – have a read what Gizmodo has to say Zune HD Review: The PMP, Evolved Technorati Tags: Zune HD Comments Anonymous September 16, 2009 I was about to order a Zune HD to replace my Zune 4. But given the new 4.0 PC client software has so many podcast related issues (I listen mainly to podcasts) then I am seriously questioning the idea... I hope they get the issues resolved...
