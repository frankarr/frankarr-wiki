Help Oscar Become A Doctor My dear friend Oscar Trimboli is kicking off the process of a doctorate at the Macquarie Graduate School of Management on " Impact of Software as a Service on Australian Independent Software Vendors " He wanted me to put a plug in my blog for his efforts. Normally, I say no to these request. Actually, that's not true. I rarely say no. I tend to say "YES" to these requests. And since he is starting the journey , I'm happy to help. So, if you are a software developer in Australia, and you would like to get involved in the research and benefit from it's findings, leave me a comment and I will hook you up with Oscar.
