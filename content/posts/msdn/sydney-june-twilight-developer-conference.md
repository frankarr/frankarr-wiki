Sydney June Twilight Developer Conference I attended the Security Summit in Sydney on Monday, which included the June Twilight MSDN Update . There is still time to register for all the other cities. I'm planning on heading down to the session in Canberra on June 18. The Twilight session was lots of fun. The room was setup with tables, dinner was provided, and the information delivered was excellent. I'm keen to see the feedback from the attendees to see if they liked the format too.
