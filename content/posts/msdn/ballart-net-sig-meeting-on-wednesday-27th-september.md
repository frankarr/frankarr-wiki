BALLART.NET SIG Meeting on Wednesday 27th September I got the word from Damian 'Tech.Ed' Edwards about the inaugural meeting of the THE NEW BALLART.NET SIG DON'T MISS THE FIRST MEETING OF THE NEW BALLART.NET SIG, WEDNESDAY 27th SEPTEMBER, 5:30PM, GREENHILL ENTERPRISE CENTRE , MOUNT HELEN "Getting up to speed with .Net" with David Lemphers from Microsoft Australia This is the first meeting of the new Ballarat.NET Special Interest Group, a regional community group for individuals who use or are interested in the Microsoft .Net software platform. In this first meeting David Lemphers from Microsoft Australia will introduce the .Net platform and lay the foundation for topics of future meetings. Even if you think you know it all already, come along, bring your questions, have a bite to eat and meet fellow .Net users in the area and help shape your future topics. Event Details Summary: When: Wednesday 27th September, 5:30pm for light dinner and drinks followed by presentation from 6:00pm to 8:00pm Where: Greenhill Enterprise Centre, Mount Helen Who: David Lemphers , Developer Envangelist, Microsoft Australia Cost: Free to registered members of Victoria.NET ( click here to register for free ) RSVP: damian.edwards at rav.vic.gov.au by Tuesday 26th September Comments Anonymous September 26, 2006 The comment has been removed Additional resources Last updated on 2006-09-19
