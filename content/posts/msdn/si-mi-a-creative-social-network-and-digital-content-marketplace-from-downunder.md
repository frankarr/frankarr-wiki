Si-Mi : A creative social network and digital content marketplace, from downunder Spotted this story in The SMH : Aussies bid to share the video wealth (thx for bring it to my attention Mickord ) what is si-mi? Si-Mi is a creative social network and digital content marketplace. Si-Mi.com is a digital media platform for users to upload, share and sell their content. On Si-Mi, users can set their price and keep 90% of the net proceeds. All content can be voted on by the Si-Mi community in a social networking environment so that the most popular content is promoted to the front page for millions of visitors to see (and buy!). Any Si-Mi user can upload and share their videos, music, podcasts, animations, ebooks, and blogs for free. Users can upload in multiple formats and downloaders can watch videos in Flash, Quicktime, Windows Media formats and download to PC, PSP, iPods and other mobile devices. A 30-second free preview is available for all, and if you like what you see, you can buy it, vote on it, comment on it, add the author to your watchlist or share with friends.
