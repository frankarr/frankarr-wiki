Eric Rudder
,
Senior Vice President. Servers and Tools

The
TechEd
Party. It was held at
Exhibition Park
in the pavillion that is used as the
National Tally Room
during elections. The theme was extreme sports, and featured a number of fun sporty activities such as Bungie Trampolines, Snow board simulator, Horizontal Bungy Run, Wall Climbing, Bouncing Areas, Inflatable slides, Bumper cars, a fine selection of party food, airhockey tables,  music and a fantastic gymnastics show. I probably missed other activies so I'm sure the attendees will add more info in my comments.
