Brian Madsen gives birth to CSharpZealot.com ... I got a ping via IM from Brian Madsen about a new community site he has pulled together - www.csharpzealot.com. CSharpZealot.com Launched... After quite a while - and some broken plans to fully customise a community site myself - www.csharpzealot.com has been launched. Obviously the community will grow as members contribute to it so since i'm currently the only member it hasn't grown yet. I'm hoping to bring some interesting focus onto my favorite language - C# and let the community grow to support itself. www.orcsweb.com was gracious enough to sponsor the hosting (can't say i'd ever be in better hands than those guys) and www.telligent.com for providing a licensed professional version of Community Server.
