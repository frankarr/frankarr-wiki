AASG : Queensland Traffic Another Australian Sidebar gadget about Queensland Traffic Cameras. This one is from the good folks at Soul Solutions Queensland Traffic View the 15 traffic cameras from around Queensland Australia. Settings allow you to choose a single camera (updates every min) or default to cycle through cameras. Undock the gadget for full resolution. Click through to view Traffic information. Nice one guys!
