Office Labs. Try. Experience. Discuss. Look. Listen. Laugh. Learn. I've been watching our internal office labs for a while now and wondering when it would see the light of day. Well, that day was this week when the covers were taken off the site : https://www.officelabs.com/ There are a few interesting projects up there Community Clips Search Commands SharePointPedia InkSeine Task Market I've been using InkSeine on my tablet for a while now. It's a prototype ink application from MSR. There are a few other projects which I hope will surface onto office labs soon.
