Australian IT : Testing the Web 2.0 water I spotted the following article this week at Australian IT: Testing the Web 2.0 water. I was surprised to see a great many of the aussie web entrepreneurs missed in the coverage. Where were the 2Web Crew ? They were conspicuous by the absence. So, let me do my bit and shine a light on some of the stuff I know. Paul Montgomery blogs about a successful meetup on Thursday in Melbourne , which featured Ben Barren.
