Tour de Kids on VE I was catching up on my feeds, and read James McCutcheon's latest post - j3/HP/Microsoft Live helps Starlight Foundation. I followed the link to the Tour de Kids site and found IMPORTANT UPDATE! You can now follow the progress of the Tour de Kids peloton in real time via GPS tracking. See we we are at this very moment! Our special thanks to Bernard Lee of Hewlett-Packard and James McCutcheon of J3Technology for making this possible. The ride may have finished yesterday, but the map is still there Nice one!
