C# Developer Opportunities for Aussies to go to NYC I got an email about a fantastic opportunity for Aussie developers (to take advantage of the E-3 Visa ) who want to spend time in the Big Apple working on cutting edge stuff: Based in New York City, we are looking to expand our staff of senior-level software engineers. Our clients include some of the world's largest financial institutions. We engage on technically challenging projects that compel us to continually evolve our skills, both individually and collectively. Our ideal candidate has the following attributes: * At least 5-10 years of professional software development experience, including using C# and .NET. * Java/J2EE/C++ experience considered desirable but not required * Strong grasp of software design patterns and effective development methodologies * Demonstrated experience writing commercial-grade software applications with a preference for thick-client apps over web-based apps * Deep understanding of multithreaded, real-time, and event-driven software architectures * Driven to produce software of the highest quality through self-discipline, clear and engaged communication, and attention to detail * BS in Computer Science or equivalent experience To be accepted for this position you will need to have a deep software background. Web experience and basic server side scripting are not sufficient. Our interview process is rigorous, and will test your knowledge of software architecture, implementation skills, understanding of process and communication abilities. We offer a highly competitive salary, full health benefits and a performance-based cash+equity bonus plan, as well as a truly great place to work. We value our people, and are committed to providing a respectful work environment with challenges appropriate to your experience and aptitude. To qualify for this position, you will need to be a US citizen, hold a US working visa, or be an Australian cititzen. You will also need to be willing to relocate to New York City. If you are interested in the role, drop me an email and I will pass your details on. * Disclosure * - I am not receiving any fees or rewards in relation to this.
