I'm Back At Last Well, after 11 weeks away from the office, I find myself back at work today. My surgery went well, as has my recovery, so today I find myself back at my home away from home. While I was in recovery, I watched heaps of dvds, read a few good books , perused many rss feeds, watched way too much daytime television, followed the ups and downs of my footy team , enjoyed the music of Eurovison, saw a few blockbuster movies , and got time to go on adventures with my wife and kids. Now, back to the reality of the job, and all the stuff we need to do for the upcoming months - we have medc, teched, pdc and a number of great products to launch. Did you miss me??
