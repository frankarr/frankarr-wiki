Seriously hooked on Fresca Ok, I'm going to admit it.  I've been keeping it quiet for a while, but it's time I came clean. I have a serious addiction to Fresca - that "distinctively crisp, moderately smooth, intriguingly citrus, totally refreshing soda". In this land of Starbucks , you would have thought I would be addicted to coffee - turns out that's not the case. I do have my morning Caffè Americano , with a shot of suger-free vanilla , but the thing that keeps me going through out the day is my Fresca. I just can't explain the addiction, but I'm hooked. Maybe it's because the drink is as old as me, or maybe it's the phenylalanine , or maybe it's something I couldn't get back in Australia which makes it all the more desirable? It could just be Fresca means "fresh" in Portuguese, Spanish and Italian . and that appeals to me Who knows.
