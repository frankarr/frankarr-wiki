In Australia, .NET hasn't got any foothold ???? There's been lots of talk in the blogosphere in relation to a recent post by Martin Fowler entitled - RubyMicrosoft . (** link fixed) I don't want to get into the whole " AlphaGeek s are moving away from the Microsoft platform" debate, but there is one comment by Martin that I want to respond to. Martin writes .... In Australia, .NET hasn't got any foothold at all amongst our clients. I'm not sure what to make of this data. We aren't so big to be a statistically valid sample on our own. But it's a useful data point nonetheless particularly since we like to think our clients are the "alpha IT shops". Martin, Did you know the Australian Architecture Forum 2007 is on in a few weeks and Thoughtworks is a sponsor of the event Did you know ThoughtWorks is a member of Australia.NET clusters together with over 650 other companies that use .NET in Australia . The client list from Thoughtworks in Australia includes companies like AMP, ANZ bank, Coca Cola, Westpac, all with significant .NET deployments. Did you know ReMix is sold out . I'm sure there are some folks coming for a stickybeak including the Alpha Geeks. I'll leave it there.
