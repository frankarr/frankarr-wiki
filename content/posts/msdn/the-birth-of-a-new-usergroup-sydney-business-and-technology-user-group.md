The birth of a new usergroup : Sydney Business and Technology User Group I've been part of an email discussion with Craig Bailey over the past few weeks in which a new user group has been born - the Sydney Business and Technology User Group. Craig has the story - BizTech: Announcing the Sydney Business & Technology User Group (SBT UG) Now, this user group is the evolution of the Sydney VFP User Group, and again Craig has the story - VFP: Important announcement concerning the Sydney VFP User Group This is a really positive move, and one that allows a place for senior developers, team leaders, software managers and software company owners to get together. Sounds like a great way forward.
