Welcome my New Starter - Jeff Alexander While I have been offline, a new member of my team started - Jeff Alexander . Jeff has joined the team as an IT Pro Evangelist, joining Michael Kleef . Jeff is a Microsoft veteran - he's been around longer than me! Have a read of his blog to find out more of this interesting fellow! These two guys are part of our new initiatives to build bridges with the IT Pro Community, in much the same was as we have done to the Developer community over the past few year.  You will see them at events, providing editorial content for the TechNet Flash newsletter, and generally being active both online & offline. Let's welcome JeffA to the team!
