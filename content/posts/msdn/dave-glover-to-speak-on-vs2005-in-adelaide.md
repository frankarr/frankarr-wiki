Dave Glover to speak on VS2005 in Adelaide Dave Glover is heading over to Adelaide next week and will be presenting all about Visual Studio 2005 to the local ADNUG members MSDN UPDATE MEETING WEDNESDAY 15th December at 12-2pm at SA Innovation Centre , Level 2, Santos House, King William Street, Adelaide Dave Glover, our Microsoft Developer Evangelist, will be giving a presentation on VS2005 Team System for Refactoring, Unit Test, Profiling, Performance Test, and management aspects of Software Development Lift Cycle. Kick off time will be Wednesday Dec 15th at 12:05, with a short break for lunch at 12:30 (not ideal, but rooms are busy), so if you can make the session please be prompt for a 12:05 start. Andrew Coates has more details about this and other ADNUG activities. If you are in Adelaide, get along and give Dave a hard time!
