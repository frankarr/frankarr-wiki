I have a number of duties at this year's event - I'm leading the panel at the Blogger Lunch, I am doing a Chalk Talk , I am working the booth, I'm doing some press interviews. It will also be my farewell to the Australian technical community. While I am at Tech.Ed, my wife will be managing the "pack and load" back home, in preparation for our relocation. To say a lot is happening this week, would be an understatement!
