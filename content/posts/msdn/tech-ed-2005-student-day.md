Tech.Ed 2005 Student Day One of the recent additions to Tech.Ed has been the student day , which is run the day before Tech.Ed begins. It features a smattering of the best sessions as well as sessions of interest to students in general. This year, it's all happening Tuesday August 30th, at the Gold Coast Convention Centre and it is open to students studying Computer Science, Software Engineering, Information Systems, Information Management and Information Technology. 10:30am Registration 11:00am Welcome Keynote 11:30am Data#3 Industry Recruitment 12:00pm Surprise Speaker 12:30pm Microsoft Recruitment 1:00pm Lunch 1:30pm The Power of Blogging - Betsy Aoki 2:00pm Windows Mobile Session - Nick Randolph 3:00pm Games Development with Microsoft XNA & Sneak Peak at Avalon - Tony Goodhew 4:00pm Gaming with Microsoft Development Tools: - Brian Keller 5:00pm Indigo Overview - Ari Bixhorn 6:00pm Finish - Buses depart venue Head over to the Tech.Ed Student Day site for more info and registration Technorati Tag: TechEdAU [ Currently Playing : Vukovi Umiru Sami - Boris Novkovic feat.
