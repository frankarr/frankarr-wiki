Responses from Where are all the developers??
Last week, I wrote a post
Where are all the
developers??
This generated
a
load of comments
, and I thank each and everyone who took the time to add
their comments
Some of the issues/concerns identified were:
cost  / time out of the office
lack of details about specific sessions
not enought content for web developers
info is all online, via blogs, webcasts, forums
the community provides information / support
However, it wasn't all negative. A number of comments were from attendees who
were looking forward to come along and be part of the action.
So, what am we going to do with this feedback? For a start, we will use
it as input for the *next* events that we put together. We need to make these
things relevant to those who attend.
FWIW - In terms of not having details about upcoming sessions -
