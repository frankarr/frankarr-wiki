I love my Palm Treo 750 !
Lots of things happened this week, and a personal highlight for me was getting a new phone - a
Palm Treo 750
.
Now, everyone knows how much
I love my iMate K-JAM
, but
the Treo just rocks
.
It's the best phone I've had - and I've had a few. And it's definitely the best Windows Mobile device I have used - and I've tried them all.
The
Palm Innovations
are really slick and really make it an easy to use phone.
The word is that
it's launching soon in Australia
, and I reckon it's going to be a winner.
What's going to happen to
my iMate K-JAM
? - I haven't decided, but most likely it's going to one of my children.
