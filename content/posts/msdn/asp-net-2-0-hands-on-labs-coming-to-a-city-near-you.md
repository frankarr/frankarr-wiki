asp.net 2.0 hands on labs coming to a city near you I conjunction with Webcentral , we are running a series of hands-on-labs around the country- Brisbane - Monday, 9 August 2004 Canberra - Friday, 13 August 2004 Melbourne - Monday, 16 August 2004 Adelaide - Wednesday, 18 August 2004 Perth - Friday, 20 August 2004 Sydney - Monday, 23 August 2004 The format is a half day, practical hands-on training sessions that will help you learn more about ASP.NET 2.0 The sessions will also involve a brief overview of WebCentral's capabilities in the .NET arena, including their 10 Second Server solutions. In each city, there will be two identical sessions during the day, Morning Session will run from 8:45am - 12:30pm Afternoon Session will run from 1:30pm - 5:15pm Register Now - Places are Strictly Limited!
