What a surprise - Mr dominate the attendees at Tech.Ed I've been wallowing around in data over the weekend, and out of curiousity I thought I would look at the split between boy geeks and girl geeks attending Tech.Ed . I was partly inspired by some of the creative flair that was sent into the Tech.Ed Flair contest. I based my analysis on the Salutation field, which does introduce some ambiguity in relation to Dr & Prof, but it gives a good indicative view. Salutation Total Mr 93.29% Ms 3.83% Mrs 1.53% Miss 0.82% Dr 0.41% Prof 0.12% Grand Total 100.00% *Sigh* really no surprises here, is there?
