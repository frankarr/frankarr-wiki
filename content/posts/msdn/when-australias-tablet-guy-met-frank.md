When Australia's Tablet Guy Met Frank I finally had a chance to catchup with Uber Tablet Geek, Hugo Ortega. I sense that Hugo had been stalking his prey for a while. He's been down to the office a few times, first to present to the Sydney .NET User Group back in January, met up with Jeff Alexander , then just recently t o present to the Sydney Windows Mobile User Group. So, he certainly knew the lay of the land. We caught up for a coffee , talked tablets, kids, schools , and other assorted geek stuff. One thing for sure, Hugo is certainly passionate about the tablet, that's for sure. He's even gone diving with one We brainstormed ideas about ways to get more people exposed to Tablet PCs and the wonderful user experience. I'm sure the brainstorms will lead to something concert in the next few months. So, Hugo it was great to meet you finally. You certainly are Australia's Tablet Guy.
