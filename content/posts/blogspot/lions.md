"Lions back in business The Brisbane Lions' quest for the 2003 premiership and a momentous third straight flag is well and truly alive after accounting for Adelaide by 42 points in Friday night’s semi-final at the Gabba. After being challenged by the Crows, the Lions showed their strength with a seven-goal final term to win 18.16 (124) to 12.10 (82).
