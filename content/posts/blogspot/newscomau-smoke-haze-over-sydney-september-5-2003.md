NEWS.com.au | Smoke haze over Sydney (September 5, 2003): "Smoke haze over Sydney September 5, 2003 SYDNEYSIDERS woke to the smell of smoke today, as firefighters continued preparations for the opening of the statutory bush fire danger period on October 1. The smoke haze over the city this morning came from a 373 hectare hazard reduction burn on the Cowan Track in Ku-ring-gai Chase National Park, a fire brigade spokesman said."
