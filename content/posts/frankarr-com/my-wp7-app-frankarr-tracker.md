I’ve been meaning to write an [Windows Phone app](http://create.msdn.com/) for ages. I even told [Brandon Watson](http://manyniches.com) that I would. 

I just never got around to it, until I saw [a tweet from Nick Hodge](https://twitter.com/#!/NickHodgeMSFT/status/59450784051511296) which inspired me to get started

[Nick](http://blogs.msdn.com/nickhodge) pointed me to the [Windows Phone 7 Social Viewer Application Template](http://blogs.msdn.com/b/dglover/archive/2011/04/08/windows-phone-7-social-viewer-application-template-released.aspx) that [Nick Randolph](http://nicksnettravels.builttoroam.com/) and [Dave Glover](http://blogs.msdn.com/b/dglover/) had put together. 

> 
The Social Viewer template is a Visual Studio template that allows you to jump start the development of a Windows Phone 7 application. It provides the foundations of an application that can aggregate multiple RSS/Atom feeds, Facebook and Twitter. 

I downloaded the zip file, looked over the documentation the guys had put together, and then I got started.

First, I got the tools installed onto my [Samsung Series 9 laptop](http://www.samsung.com/us/computer/laptops/NP900X3A-A03US). 

- Installed [Visual Studio 2010 Express for Windows Phone](http://create.msdn.com/en-us/home/getting_started)
Install [January 2011 Windows Phone Tools Update](http://www.microsoft.com/downloads/en/details.aspx?displaylang=en&FamilyID=49b9d0c5-6597-4313-912a-f0cca9c7d277) 

Next, I fired up Visual Studio, opened up the Social Viewer App Template and got coding.

Then I got my kids involved to design the app icon. 

Emma suggested I put “Tech Frank” onto a radar image – so off I went.

I futzed with [Paint.Net](http://www.getpaint.net/) for a while trying to get the image just right. Dave was right – the icon is the thing that took the longest time!

Bill gave me a few pointers and voila – application icon created!

So what does the app do? 

Well, you’re going to have to [install it](http://social.zune.net/redirect?type=phoneApp&id=e89f6f20-d36f-e011-81d2-78e7d1fa76f8) and find out for yourself

Enjoy!! Who knows, you may find [FrankArr](http://frankarr.com/) in your neck of the woods!
