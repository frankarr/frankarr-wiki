Ok, so I’m on a roll! Last week I got my first #WP7 App out. This week , I grabbed the v2 of the Social Viewer Template for Windows Phone 7 from codeplex and put together another windows phone 7 app, which I called Saints Footy . It has twitter feeds , feeds from news sites and fan sites , and the facebook feed from saints.com.au – the best way to experience Saints Footy on Windows Phone!! Now, if only the Saints would win a few more games…. Big shout out to Nick Randolph and Dave Glover for a wonderful job with the Social Viewer Template.
