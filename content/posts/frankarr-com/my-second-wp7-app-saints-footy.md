[](https://frankarr.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/05/stkildafc.png)Ok, so I’m on a roll! Last week I got [my first #WP7 App](http://frankarr.com/2011/04/28/my-wp7-app-frankarr-tracker/) out. 

This week , I grabbed the v2 of the [Social Viewer Template for Windows Phone 7](http://socialviewer.codeplex.com/) from [codeplex](http://codeplex.com) and put together another windows phone 7 app, which I called [Saints Footy](http://social.zune.net/redirect?type=phoneApp&id=a08a539f-6374-e011-81d2-78e7d1fa76f8). 

It has [twitter feeds](http://twitter.com/#!/stkildafc), [feeds](http://www.saints.com.au/) from [news sites](http://www.heraldsun.com.au/sport/afl/clubs/st-kilda) and [fan sites](http://www.redwhiteandblack.com.au), and the [facebook feed](http://www.facebook.com/home.php#!/stkfc) from [saints.com.au](http://www.saints.com.au) – the best way to experience [Saints Footy](http://social.zune.net/redirect?type=phoneApp&id=a08a539f-6374-e011-81d2-78e7d1fa76f8) on Windows Phone!! 

Now, if only the [Saints](http://www.saints.com.au/) would win a few more games….

Big shout out to [Nick Randolph](http://nicksnettravels.builttoroam.com/) and [Dave Glover](http://blogs.msdn.com/b/dglover/) for a wonderful job with the [Social Viewer Template.](http://socialviewer.codeplex.com/)
