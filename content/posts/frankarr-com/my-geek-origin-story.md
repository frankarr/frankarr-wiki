[Michael Kordahi](http://delicategeniusblog.com/?), aka [Delicate Genius](http://delicategeniusblog.com/), is asking for [Geek Origin Stories](http://delicategeniusblog.com/?p=1292).

How can I not contribute to this worthy cause……

> 
[](https://frankarr.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/08/cbc-1981.jpg)The year was 1981, and my high school, [CBC StKilda](http://www.cbcstkilda.com), got a fancy new [Cromenco](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cromemco) computer system. I was asked to man the computer lab by my math teacher, Mrs Fagin, and to pass the time, I wrote programs in Structured Basic. 

I remember putting in programs that would to print out ASCII art – playboy bunny logo and Alfred E. Newman stick in my memory. After spending time playing in that lab, I decided to following a computing path, even though I had been planning to go on and do medicine. 

[](https://frankarr.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/08/punchcard.jpg)I signed up for the [EDP](http://www.infotech.monash.edu.au/about/schools/clayton/history/csse-caulfield.html) (or Electronic Data Processing) course at [CIT](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chisholm_Institute_of_Technology) in 1982. In my first year we used [punch cards](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punched_card) to input out programs. I still have some those punch cards someone in the garage – I’ve carried them for almost 30 years! In my second year, the class had access to time sharing terminals connected to a [PR1ME](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_Computer) computer and we worked on PR1ME Information (which was similar to [PICK](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pick_operating_system)), as well as [COBOL](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COBOL) and [FORTRAN](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fortran), and in my third year, we had a dedicated [Data General](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data_General) system, we programmed in [COBOL](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COBOL) and [PL/1](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PL/1) and we had access to a new fangled a [IBM PC](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IBM_PC) and a software package called KnowledgeMan for our group project.

After graduating, I entered the workforce as a humble programmer and my first job was converting FORTRAN applications for an engineering company on St Kilda Rd, who were migrating systems. That was the start of many projects during my time 

So there you have it….
