I’ve said it before, and I’ll say it again.

[1991](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1991) was a big year for me. 

I got married. 

I bought a house. 

My first child was born 

AND 

I joined Microsoft on [May 20](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/May_20), [1991](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1991) 

Today marks my [20th](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/20_(number)) year [anniversary](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wedding_anniversary) at Microsoft. 

As I have done for the past few years ([19](http://blogs.msdn.com/b/frankarr/archive/2010/05/23/only-19.aspx), [18](http://blogs.msdn.com/frankarr/archive/2009/05/20/dix-huit-ans-aujourd-***-18-pounds.aspx), [17](http://blogs.msdn.com/frankarr/archive/2008/05/20/17-xvii-1001-big-day.aspx), [16](http://blogs.msdn.com/frankarr/archive/2007/05/20/my-sweet-16th-16-sweet-years-at-microsoft.aspx). [15](http://blogs.msdn.com/frankarr/archive/2006/05/20/602294.aspx), [14](http://blogs.msdn.com/frankarr/archive/2005/05/20/420390.aspx), [13](http://blogs.msdn.com/frankarr/archive/2004/05/20/136267.aspx)), time to reflect a bit on my time at Microsoft. Yes, I still *love* the job. 

The resume stays the same, but I’m [now back in Australia, based in Melbourne](http://blogs.msdn.com/b/frankarr/archive/2011/03/21/wait-what.aspx).

This year, I find myself in a different situation — I don’t have an office, so no 20lbs of M&Ms sitting in a bowl outside my door to share with my work colleagues. 

It’s kinda weird that I have no one to share the moment with. It’s just another day. 

But, I did get a rather large and heavy parcel sent to me. 

[*](https://frankarr.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/05/wp_000347.jpg)

which had another box inside

[](https://frankarr.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/05/wp_000348.jpg)

and inside that box was another box addressed to me

[](https://frankarr.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/05/wp_000349.jpg)

it was like a [Babushka doll](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matryoshka_doll)!!*

So – what’s in it?

Well – it’s my 20 year “crystal” from Microsoft. (yeah – crappy photo, I know. I’ll take another one in the morning)

[](https://frankarr.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/05/wp_000350.jpg)

So – 20 years down, and the future looks promising – [skype is joining the family](http://www.microsoft.com/Presspass/press/2011/may11/05-10CorpNewsPR.mspx), [kinect is going gang busters](http://blogs.technet.com/b/microsoft_blog/archive/2011/05/12/kinect-sports-amp-dance-central-help-keep-xbox-360-in-the-no-1-spot.aspx) with [it’s own SDK](http://research.microsoft.com/en-us/um/redmond/projects/kinectsdk/) , [windows phone is getting Angry Birds soon](http://windowsteamblog.com/windows_phone/b/windowsphone/archive/2011/05/18/must-have-games-kicks-off-may-25-angry-birds-arrives-june-29.aspx). 

Seems like there’s a lots to still do, and I’m pretty excited to continue doing it.

Plus, now that I’m back in Australia – I can get along to some local events, such as [DDD Melbourne](http://dddmelbourne.com/), [PREMIX](http://www.noisetosignal.com.au/remix/#premix) & [REMIX](http://www.noisetosignal.com.au/remix/)

Bring on the next 20! Maybe I’ll get some M&Ms just for me and the kids!…
