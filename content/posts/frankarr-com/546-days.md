Today marks 18 months I've been at Telstra. Feels just like the other day I posted about [12 Months In](http://frankarr.com/2015/01/13/12-months-in/)

The last 18 months is best summed up like this – the first 6 months was spent working on the API strategy, the next 6 months was to sell the strategy, and the last 6 months to implement. So we now find ourselves at the start of the next phase.

We've also been a bit public over the last few months, first at [APIDays in Sydney](http://www.apidays.io/), and then [I ♥ APIS](http://iloveapisapac.com/), which means we got some coverage:

- [Telstra opens first public API to developers](http://www.itnews.com.au/News/400351,telstra-opens-first-public-api-to-developers.aspx)
- [#I ♥ APIS](http://tel.st/iloveapis)
- [The rise of API-ification within the Australian and New Zealand startup ecosystem](http://www.startupdaily.net/2015/07/rise-api-ification-within-australian-new-zealand-startup-ecosystem/)
- [Inside Telstra's shift to an 'API first' culture](https://ia.acs.org.au/news/inside-telstras-shift-to-an-api-first-culture-400532)
