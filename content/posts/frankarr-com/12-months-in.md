It's been [12 months since I started with Telstra](http://frankarr.com/2014/01/14/new-year-new-house-new-job/). My pass still worked after a few weeks off, so that's a good sign.

Telstra Telstra Telstra loves APIS

Anyways…. looking back on the past 12 months, lots happened …..

- the group I joined [changed names from GAP to TSG](http://www.zdnet.com/article/new-company-for-telstras-emerging-tech-ambitions/)
- got to meet some [wonderful folks at muru-d](https://muru-d.com/startups/)
- helped out with the pilot for [codeclub australia](http://www.codeclubau.org/)
- met a [bunch of passionate API folks](http://www.meetup.com/API-Marketing-Strategy/)
- went to SF for the [I ♥ APIs 2014 conference](https://blog.apigee.com/detail/get_digital_ready_now_i_apis_2014_kicks_off)
- created the [#stickercolumn](https://twitter.com/search?f=realtime&q=%23stickercolumn&src=typd)
- was a guest on [the 100th edition of the franklyspeaking podcast ](http://franklyspeakingpodcast.com/fs100-finally-with-frank-arrigo/)
- spent time with lots of API platform vendors – which has been strange for me, as I spent such a long time with a technology vendor
- got to work with a bunch of really smart people
- plus a bunch of other stuff that has made the past 12 months zoom by!

Enough looking back – time to look forward. Onward to the next 12.

So far the job has been pretty much inward looking, as we worked to get a API platform in place & get some APIs on-boarded to the platform.

But that's all about about to change!

I have an upcoming talk at [APIDays Sydney](http://syd.apidays.io/), titled "Telstra's Path to API Enlightenment" and I'll use that to describe the journey we've been on. (oh, and speaking of journey, I saw a [great tweet recently by Aussienick](https://twitter.com/aussienick/status/552254593367691264) which describes a similar journey)
