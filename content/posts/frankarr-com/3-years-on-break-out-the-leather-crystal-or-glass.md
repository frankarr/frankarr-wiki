During the last 12 months, I've gone from working on [the Telstra API Strategy](http://www.computerworld.com.au/article/603929/telstra-api-revolution/) to moving to the Innovation team, in the Chief Technology Office.

Along the way, I picked up new duties and am responsible for people again.   I really like being a "people leader", helping develop, coach, mentor, inspire. I even had a 'grad' to tend to and [Serina](https://au.linkedin.com/in/serina-gill-83034b96) (aka [gilly](https://twitter.com/serinagill)) has been absolute delight. Maybe you have read some of her stories — [GovHack 2016](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/govhack-2016-serina-gill?trk=mp-reader-card), [My week with … A bot](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/my-week-bot-serina-gill?trk=mp-reader-card), [Techfugees for Young people: #hack4refugees](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/techfugees-young-people-hack4refugees-serina-gill?trk=mp-reader-card), [A world of code](https://exchange.telstra.com.au/2016/11/30/a-world-of-code/). Her time with me is coming to an end, so I wish Serina all the best with the next step of her career.

I really like working with grads, and since [Telstra has a well structured graduate program](https://careers.telstra.com/Graduates) I look forward to contributing to that.

Something else I like, is being involved with [Muru-D](http://www.muru-d.com/), and last year [#SYD3](https://muru-d.com/startups/sydney/) was full of amazing young companies. I got to work closely with [Simpla](https://muru-d.com/startups/profile/simpla/) and [Cuberider](https://muru-d.com/startups/profile/quberider/) – seriously, how amazing was it to see [Cuberider send Australia's first payload to the ISS](http://www.smh.com.au/technology/sci-tech/cuberider-sends-australias-first-payload-to-the-international-space-station-20161209-gt7o1d.html)!!  I was involved in the recent SYD4 bootcamp and look forward to seeing the next batch come through! And what a legacy [Annie Parker](https://twitter.com/annie_parker) is leaving behind, but can't wait to see what [Lighthouse](https://www.lighthouse.sydney/) becomes!

There's a bit of theme – one of nurturing, mentoring, curating, advising & inspiring and these are all the things that get me going and inspire me. So, can't complain about that!

Oh and the leather, crystal, glass reference – it's the suggested three year anniversary gifts
